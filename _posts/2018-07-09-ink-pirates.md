---
title: Ink Pirates
date: 2018-07-09 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [writing, darkness, poetry, pain, struggle, creative-process]
---

Mere flesh wounds...  
But gory ghashes in actuality  
Letter by letter, cut by cut  
Word by word, sewing my lips  
I can only crawl, silently bawl  
As blank verses corner me.  
Dreams kissing vanishing point,  
A rude awakening into gray reality,  
Similes, metaphors, black and white.  
Dusk and dawn both alike.  
Page by page I drown in past  
Enjoying the literary assassination  
Meek body, washed on a bleak shore  
You'll have me, the prey of ink pirates.
