---
title: Over The Fire
date: 2023-04-26 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [resilience, truth, strength, inner-turmoil, perseverance, darkness]
---

When the rules wear out  
And the systems fail,  
There's nothing to follow,  
Cold goes every trail,  

Set aside your desires  
And walk over the fire.  

When stars die and fade  
Leaving behind ghosts  
Of their last light, lost—  
Scintillations by the coast,  

Let the waves rise higher,  
And walk over the fire.  

Euphonic lies fall  
In the ears each day.  
Let the truth reign  
Over the deep dismay.  

Extract honesty from liars,  
Make them walk over the fire.  

Hidden no longer  
Demons living within  
Living no longer  
Innocence on outer skin.  

Retrospect, revive and rewire  
And walk over the fire.
