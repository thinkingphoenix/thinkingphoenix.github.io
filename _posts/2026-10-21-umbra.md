---
title: Umbra
date: 2026-10-21 02:07:44 +0530
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [alternative side, darkness, insanity]
---

The umbra  
Of your shadow,  
Your darkness,  
I caress  
With my nails.  
I can see you   
With eyes closed;  
Your absence prevails.  

Light just never falls on you,  
It falls off you,  
Just how I do.  
 The night mist  
On the vines of  
Your lips,  
Make me twist.  
I fear your bare body,  
You fear my bare soul.  
Your hollowness,  
I caress  
With my nails.  
I can feel you  
With eyes closed,   
In your thoughts; I'm the deranged.  

Your voice, particularly  
Frigid in the night,  
Stirs in me  
A dampened, deprecated longing  
That gradually grows nuclear-  
I grab and claw my face in fear.  
That's what happens when  
My body is too confined to define  
The cold you leave in my sky,    
Akin to when a bright star dies.  
My senses and sensory organs   
Can barely let you know this.  
I could  
crawl, limp, stagger, sprint, trudge  
From the entirety of you.  
And it would never be enough  
In my insanity of you.  

On your bedroom floor,  
On your kitchen door,  
On the pavement, the ground and trees,  
And on my body as you walk away from me,  

The penumbra  
Of your shadow,  
Your shape,  
I traced  
With my fingers.  
I wanted to love you   
Like you'd never known,  
And turn you into glitter.  

Now the umbra  
Of your shadow,  
Your numbness  
I caress  
With my nails.  
It hurts that I love you  
And you've never known.  
And I have yet again failed.
