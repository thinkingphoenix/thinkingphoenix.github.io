---
title: The Flowers that Entered my Home
date: 2026-08-14 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [flowers, heartbreak, reality, friendship, reflection]
---

It was a Thursday morning.  
I was in ecstasy.  
I was on the shore of love.  
So close, so far.  

You see, I don't find bouquets symbolic.  
Take me to a garden and I'd placidly admire  
The bloom, the chirping birds, squirrels in frolic.   

But since my friends decided it's most becoming   
Of a man to bring flowers when for the first time   
He meets a woman of interest,  
I let them in this shenanigan, invest.  

And they raced on their motorcycle,  
Asked their lady friends on what colors to pick.  
They planned for me, rejoiced for me.  
I turned gray and grayer under my skin.  
Unbeknownst to them,  
I caught a glimpse of her soul.  
And there was no home for me.  
Not the kind I’d told myself I’d see, at least.  

Wasn't her fault either.  
She never asked for flowers.  
She never asked for my devotion.  
It was all in my head,  
In my musing hours,  
That I'd tied her name to blissful emotions.  

I lied to my friends,  
I asked her to play the part for a minute.  
I never did meet her that day,  
Neither did she, choosing not to see me wither.  
But wither flowers do,   
Just like humans,  
Although we’d keep them in water.   
Just like humans,  
Who do the same with ornaments, makeup and filters.  

As always, reality finds a way to disillusion me.  
My yearning ends here, with my exit from this dream.  
Just how I survived before, I shall continue to,  
Locking away what I feel, inside my internal quarantine.  
Severing connections, wire by wire from  
My soul.  
Wires of  
passion,  
intoxication,  
desire,  
to name a few that  
lead to dangerous situations.  
I will let the wire for   
Care and kinship remain   
And reset myself in this manner.  
For I have no other way to heal.  
For God owes me tears; I'm unable to weep.   
An aching chest at occasions I have but  
I'd rather not long for what isn't mine to keep.  

I'd bury the flowers but  
They are in fact a memento  
Of my prized friendships.  
They do not have to know  
What today changed in me.  

The actuation of my theories.  

Such is the story  
Of the flowers that entered my home.  
I hold no regret,  
Except for how they'll rot soon.  
It is a Thursday afternoon.  
I skin my memory  
With surgical cuts.  
Much gore, much scars.  
I'm returning from the shore of love.  
So close, so far.
