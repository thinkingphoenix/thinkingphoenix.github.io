---
title: Pond of Blood
date: 2017-09-07 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [darkness, grief, heartbreak, solitude, inner-conflict, despair]
---

Will you come with me  
To the pond of blood?  
That I visit in every dream  
To pay homage to the  
Broken bonds of love.  

Where withered lilies whisper  
In vain against the violent wind.  
Where all your fears find shelter  
And you need not hide your armor's chink  
But only stand; feet held by water  
Only see; the skies getting darker  

And evaluate all loss  
Laughing, knowing not what to feel  
At the end of the war  
Where you were the ally and the enemy.  

There will be no dead-ends  
Or myths like peace  
That may ever obstruct.  
But the question is, my friend  
That will you come with me  
To the pond of blood?
