title: Antonyms
date: 2019-08-19 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [contrasts, duality, life, dark, pain, struggle]

Open world,  
Locked-down doors.  
Blithe faces,  
Bleeding gores.  
Warm bodies,  
Frozen cores.  
Bloody hands,  
Shining floors.   
Rising seas,  
Silent shores.  
You've had enough...  
You want no more.
