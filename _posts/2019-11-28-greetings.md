---
title: Greetings
date: 2019-11-28 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [loneliness, apathy, darkness, heartbreak, superficiality, isolation]
---

Mornings, evenings, nights,  
People and their greetings drop by  
In your dark chamber, like faint lights  
Of stars strewn in black skies.  

Some stay, like a salve on the wounds,  
Some fade; too early, too soon  
Just like how everyone  
Is busy weaving their own tales.  
Just like how everyone  
Pretends and pretends to illuminate,  

Leaving you darker, bleeding,  
Wondering what to do with  
These apathetic greetings.
