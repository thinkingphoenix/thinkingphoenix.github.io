---
title: Puppet Dance
date: 2019-10-16 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [nonet, life, control, routine, struggles, isolation]
---

When the day's carnival is over,  
We sweep our skin to look for wounds  
And scream out some of our rants,  
Hushed out, like everyday.  
Strings linked to our limbs;  
With the soul, clamped.  
Life is a  
Puppet  
Dance.
