title: The Sorceress
date: 2026-08-07 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]

Serenity. I know this word.  
I could define it with accuracy.  
But how many times have I felt it?  
Felt it, truly, not by my brain’s deceit?  
I'm so cautious I monitor my gait.  
Because how else am I to survive  
The monstrosity, ubiquitous,  
The monstrosity called fate.  
And yet, injected into my bloodstream  
Is this familiar, almost nostalgic chemical-  
Dopamine? Because I feel close to “happy”.  
Serotonin? Because I feel satiated.  
Endorphine? My pain feels nullified.  
Or…adrenaline?   
Because storms rise within me,  
Leaving angst in their wake.  
I reopen my eyes and I'm wading  
Through still, sunlit waters of a quiet lake.  
The cycle repeats itself   
Within me, as if ancient, arcane,  
Beyond my own comprehension,  
Beyond descriptions or names.    

What spell have you cast on me?  
Tell me, you bewitching Sorceress.  
How come your hands are inside my chest,  
A territory that cannot be accessed?   
I see how dexterously you've stripped off  
My chainmail, helmet, armor and shields.  
I'm bare naked from apocalypse-ready.  
How’re you able to turn my skin warm  
and my insides glow?   
Do you have runes in your fingers?  
Do you enchant your words?  
Have sirens blessed your voice?  
Is your mind a portal to other worlds?  
Tell me, Sorceress,  
For I can't resist your spells.  
I sit in my destroyed fortress.  
Sleepless, overwhelmed.
