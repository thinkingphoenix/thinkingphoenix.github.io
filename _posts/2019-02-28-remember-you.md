---
title: Remember You
date: 2019-02-28 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [denial, heartbreak, memories, longing, sorrow]
---

Remember you? I don't.  
I don't watch you from a distance with a sigh  
I don't weep in the nights and wipe my eyes  
I don't chuckle recalling your cute jokes  
I don't draw your avatars on my physics notes  
I don't utter your name in my illusioned dreams  
I don't miss our walks by those rainy streets.  
Tell the truth? I won't.  
Remember you? I don't.
