---
title: Unwiped
date: 2019-07-17 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [dark, tears, pain, secrets, suffering, internal-conflict]
---

Slow, slow... slower,  
Let tears gush out, flood your eyes over.  
Do not hush, someone may hear.  
Do not let them see you sear.  
Muffle, muffle, muffle screams more  
Suck the crimson off your gore.  
Contusions outside, confusions within  
Cold heat eats, from bone to skin.  
Burnt embers of midnight stories  
Churned within the head, you rot slowly.  
Let them flow, leave them unwiped  
They'll be salt later, hidden to sight.
