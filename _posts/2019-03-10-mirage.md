---
title: Mirage
date: 2019-03-10 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [mirage, illusion, heartbreak, desert, longing]
---

I, the bedouin, sifted through  
The sands of your heart, the Thar.  
Only realms of dunes came in view;  
Oases of love, quite far  
Caught my gaze  
I urged my pace.  
Forgetting it was a sabotage  
A god-damned mirage.
