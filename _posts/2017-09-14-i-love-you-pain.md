---
title: I Love You, Pain
date: 2017-09-14 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [pain, addiction, solitude, heartbreak, darkness, grief]
---

I love you, Pain.  
You've always been by my side  
Sometimes you gush out from my eyes  
Making me realize how fucked up is my life.  
I love you, Pain.  
How sincere have you been to me  
In the crevices of my heart you creep  
With beautiful blackouts, you help me sleep.  
I love you, Pain.  
In my loneliness, you whisper in my ear  
My worthlessness, but how much you hold me dear  
My dead dreams, you show me clear.  
I love you, Pain.  
My splintered heart still beats on  
Choking on tears, my soul breathes on  
With your blessings, this cycle repeats on.  
I love you, Pain.  
And I know you'll be there forever to hug  
I embrace you, make you my new drug  
You give me peace, unlike the crap called love.  
It's too late, I'm left insane  
I love YOU, Pain.
