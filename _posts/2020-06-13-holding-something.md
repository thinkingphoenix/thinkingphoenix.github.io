---
title: Holding Something
date: 2020-06-13 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [longing, warmth, comfort, love, connection, intimacy]
---

On some nights, I visit my terrace  
Draping the blanket of a million stars,  
I glare up high, as if seeking an answer  
To why it hurts, oh hurts my human heart  
Dear, they touch your body, your body merely  
And will nobody remember who you were  
Or how lovingly they held you, oh spare me   
Lies, bitter lies are all that I've heard.  

Someone held me different today  
I felt more than a body in those arms.  
How I wished to be wrapped in them   
Everyday as I stop the alarm.
