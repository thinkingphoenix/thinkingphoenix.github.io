---
title: No One
date: 2018-01-02 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [heartbreak, loneliness, pain, betrayal, despair, solitude]
---

No one ever understood  
I thought at least you would  
Have seen through the laughter  
The emptiness that echoes within.  
That one folly and your leaving me in  
The pitch of the ditch of desolation  
As my soul in wait  
Counts my days  
To my cremation to attain salvation.  
But you could never, oh never  
Ever feel my pain.  
How I hate being alone  
And wiping my tears in the dark  
How I hate listening to radios  
Playing songs of love and broken hearts.
