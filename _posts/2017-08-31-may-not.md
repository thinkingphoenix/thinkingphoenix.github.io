---
title: May... Not
date: 2017-08-31 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [heartbreak, pain, detachment, torture, grief, yearning]
---

Pour poison into my eyes  
So that I may not  
See you face.  

Stitch my lips together  
So that I may not  
Speak your name.  

Chain my feet to a wall  
So that I may not  
Reach your place.  

And  
Burn heart to ashes  
So that I may not  
Love you again.
