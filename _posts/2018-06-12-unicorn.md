---
title: Unicorn
date: 2018-06-12 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [fantasy, innocence, humanity, reflection, nature, escape]
---

Cotton clouds on cerulean sky  
Fir trees on solid ground  
Glistening eyes and feathery pinion  
A convoluted tale, I'm spellbound.  

Stands before me, tangible grandeur  
Yet humble enough to bow  
And offer me a ride on it's back  
The Unicorn has me in awe.  

"Can we meet in the real world too?"  
It questioned the sand in my eyes,  
"Our world is ineffably tainted"  
Disdain-stricken, I had to reply.  

"They turn swords amongst themselves   
Their malice alien to your cherubic mind  
They'll dichotomize you, or kill you for flesh  
Expect clemency?They eat their own kind."  

The petrified creature won't speak,  
But belched out in fear-tinted disgust  
"Your oneiric lands are enough to fly in"  
It said; we flew, cutting wind's gust.
