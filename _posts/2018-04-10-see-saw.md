---
title: See Saw
date: 2018-04-10 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [childhood, memories, growing-up, life, nostalgia, passage-of-time]
---

Up and down,  
You and me,  
Our tiny feet  
In the air,  
On the ground.  

Up and down,  
With a breeze  
I fell off, broke milk teeth—  
Great despair!  
...and jeans dusty brown.  

Up and down,  
Life will be,  
With burdens too heavy,  
And next in snare...  
The younger ones around.
