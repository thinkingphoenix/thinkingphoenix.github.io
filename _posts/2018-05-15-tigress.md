---
title: Tigress
date: 2018-05-15 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [nature, strength, power, majestic, wild, respect]
---

Amid the viridescent leaves  
A low growl I hear, sotto-voce  
The night skies with day-like sheen  
Blanket us both; we're not lonely  
Before her fiery eyes, I'm no more  
But a paltry mannequin.  
Her grandeur blurs my vision,  
I can't tell night from din.  
One step a time, she emerges  
From thin mist, I descry  
Before my incompetence, she lets out a belch;  
She tramples chivalrous masculine pride.  
Black over orange, stripes  
We was never meant to be harnessed…  
An epitome of nature's might,  
She's the royal tigress.
