---
title: Imagined Intimacy
date: 2022-11-20 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [intimacy, passion, desire, romance, soul, dream]
---

As our warm breaths fall on each other,  
Trading stories of their own,  
Through you, I hold the universe in my arms.  
Eyes closed, yet I'm flummoxed,  
For I behold the elegance  
Of your soul, the abode of inexplicable charm.  

As our fingers of their own accord intertwine,  
Winding and unwinding in sync with,  
Our heartbeats surging each second,  
Sublime symphonies seize the silence  
As a mellifluous heave escapes  
From your lips that mine beckon.
