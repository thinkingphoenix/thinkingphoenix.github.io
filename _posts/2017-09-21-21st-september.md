---
title: 21st September
date: 2017-09-21 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [memories, heartbreak, rain, nostalgia, music, loss]
---

It was 21st of September,  

A day I can't ever forget.  

I don't need to remember  

The rusty joy on my heart, etched.  

That day it rained heavily,  

Like now my eyes do.  

She sang a song for me  

That now haunts as I try to  

Bury my head beneath the covers  

And believe it was all a damn dream,  

The fuming vibes and the vivid colors  

As I walked beside her like a narrow stream  

By the edge of a river, a life-giver.  

I found myself losing  

Into the perpetual-seeming music  

Of the togetherness  

Of our hearts.  

But what seemed endless  

Was a melody in just two bars.  

(Where my story began, if not ours)  

My poor soul, much it pleaded  

But the song simply ended.  

And my love lives in it's simmer,  

When my heart never bled.  

It was 21st of September.  

A day I can't​ ever forget.
