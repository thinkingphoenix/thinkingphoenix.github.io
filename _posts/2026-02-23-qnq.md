I walk alone, a misfit  
Through this bizarre maze  
That we call the world—  
In which every gaze  
That meets mine is obsessed  
With perfection, standards,  
Criteria to conform to  
With a heart that longs to  
Be held, cherished,  
But is abandoned, perished.  
Why? Love at first sight maybe?  
-Isn't that how animals breed?  
What does it take?  
-Is it 6 digits of income?  
-A man who is handsome?  
-A woman with white flesh?  
-Or with supple breasts?  
We're hamsters chasing carrots  
Sprinting to death,  
On the wheel of despair  
While we question ourselves  
Why there's no one to care.  
Born to be a gem,  
We tie our worth to  
Validation by people  
Who can't push through  
The barrier of their eyes.  
-Yet we wonder why.  
There's no fucking answer  
Except to try.  
To find the correct sync of your mind.  
For the right voyagers  
Know when they a treasure find.
