---
title: The Next Fix
date: 2026-08-23 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [disillusionment, addiction, breakdown, remorse]
---
Aren’t we all addicts  
Waiting for our next fix?  

Why would it matter  
How beyond repair I am,  
If I can walk amid you all,  
Hide the bruises of my fall
And wear synthetic joy?  
Just like the toy  
I am. I will provide comfort  
And then find my place in the dirt  
Again. I do this to myself.  
Again. Over and over.  
No one at fault but me.  
My greed  
For the next jolt of ecstasy  
Injected into my veins.  
My greed  
To feel with this heart atrophied,  
Something other than hate and rage.  
I like the taste  
Of passion,  
Of affection,  
Of beauty beyond my gaze.  
I almost salivate  
As they ameliorate  
My insanity-  
But the illusion breaks too soon.  
FUCK.  
The effects start wearing off.  
Leaving familiar anguish.  
Leaving my soul tarnished.  
Leaving heaviness tenfolds the gravity.  
Leaving me farther from humanity.  
Leaving grayscaled tunnel vision.  
Leaving on me invisible incisions.  
I find myself on the ground;  
A complete blackout.  
Trembling, I pick myself up  
And lie flat again.  
I made it to the next fix in time.  
I wake up, fit and fine.  
Back to my apathy,  
Back to injuries that never bleed.  
An organism perfected for this environment,  
I never fall weak, I never weep.  
This is the hell chosen by me.  
As long as I can run fast enough  
And never look behind,  
Survive another day I will,  
As long as another fix I can find.  

If my guns jam,  
I will fight with sticks.  
I don’t need my wounds licked.   

As long as I don’t overdose  
And stay within my limits,  
I will safely remain the addict,  
Waiting for his next fix.
