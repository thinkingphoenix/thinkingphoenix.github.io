---
title: Sleep Travelogues
date: 2020-01-31 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [dreams, love, healing, comfort, imagination, escape]
---

Pretty eyes with glasses, go to sleep, go to sleep  
Put dreams into your pockets, some for me, some for me  
With words, I'm reckless, I'm falling down, falling down  
My, I feel so weightless, on solid ground, solid ground  
Call me from a payphone, when you reach there, reach there  
We'll be lost together there, floating mid-air, mid-air  
The pain will be taken, from your heart, from your heart  
And crafted into roses, skillful art, skillful art  
Your smile will be real, a melting one, melting one  
I'll be watching you ... heavily stunned, heavily stunned  
They won't ever know, the cruel world, cruel world  
But there'll be joy, 'cause we're not concerned, not concerned  
Tincture of love rubbed, on hurting wounds, hurting wounds  
I'll sit beside ...won't be going soon, going soon  
Imagination is all this. Never true, never true  
But just might it exist, with me and you, me and you
