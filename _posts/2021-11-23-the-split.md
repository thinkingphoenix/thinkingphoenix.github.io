---
title: The Split
date: 2021-11-23 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [distance, silence, heartbreak, memory, isolation]
---

Open arms;  
Empty space to embrace.  
Cold within;  
Standing under the sun's gaze.  

Spilled memories;  
Too tired to pick them back.  
Killed melodies;  
Vivid emotions turned black.  

Fifty centimetres;  
Yet two worlds apart.  
Unspoken words;  
And a stranger too close to heart.  

Negativity;  
An invisible assailant.  
Escapism;  
A truth so blatant.
