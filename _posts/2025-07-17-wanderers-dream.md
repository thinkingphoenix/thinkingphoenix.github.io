---
title: Wanderer's Dream
date: 2025-07-17 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [love, romance, dream, longing]
---

Walking through scorched Earth,  
Skin burning under the Sun,  
I have travelled on and on  
Throughout my life without shelter,  
Through burning cities and their embers,  
Through remains of what they called love,  
Through crowds of people   
Who slit throats of whom they sleep with,  
Through corrupt lanes of feelings,  
Rummaging for emotions in empty sinks,  
I stumbled upon your doorstep  
And found welcoming eyes that gleam,  
I am living and breathing in  
What's a wanderer's dream.  

More than I can express  
Or show on my face,  
My heart knows within, how safe   
It feels in the way you hold it close,  
Safe from the fierce world, warm and cherished  
More than my being could ever imagine.  
My grey eyes have been colored and embellished  
With dainty beads of happiness all over.  
The calmness that follows your voice  
That ends a bad day into a serene, singing night  
Adds more purpose to my existent life.  
Sometimes I should remember to  
Appreciate things that can't be seen,  
And only felt deep within  
What's a wanderer's dream.
