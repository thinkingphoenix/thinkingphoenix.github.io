---
title: Dear Winter
date: 2018-02-06 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [winter, solitude, pain, coldness, heartbreak, despair]
---

Dear winter,  
You've come at the right time  
When I'm cold from the insides.  
I'd blend better with you  
As this weather suits fine, too.  
Now tears freeze beneath the eyes  
And lips too sealed to say goodbyes  
To the ones parting away without smiles  
Who once lived in all of the heart's divides  
Breath in chills and staggerin' wills synchronize  
Agonies that kill with the mist which's still, harmonize  
Making me crawl to a corner of my bed's side  
In nightmares that flip the pages of my life  
When I was cold from the insides  
(Now colder all this while)  
Dear winter,  
You've come at the right time.
