---
title: The Warning
date: 2026-01-17 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [vulnerability, desire, companionship]
---

My thoughts are jumbled.  
I don an armour but I'm not invincible.  
Solitude is my forever companion,  
The only space  where I can let my guard down.  
I've let people enter at times,  
Only for disasters to unfold, only for suffering to be found.  
I've exhausted myself  
Just to let know I care.  
I've exhausted others,  
When they notice my disrepair.  
I've seen conversations die,  
Faith fade, 
People resent me  
For not loving them in their ways.  
The idea of someone within my head again  
Sends shivers down my spine,  
For what they want to see is beauty,  
Not this dark, decrepit heart of mine.  
I live, eat, walk amongst the mundane  
With isolation deep within.   
Ones who would understand me  
Would have scars too, under their skin.  
I keep convincing myself to grow  
To prosper, to achieve what I will.  
But the void within continues   
To consume me and consume me still.  
My vulnerabilities manifest into hands,  
Caressing the words you've written,  
Reading them like scriptures.  
Witnessing through the destruction,  
Standing amid the embers and splinters.  
I give to you my withered flowers,  
Trampled upon and left to rot.  
Maybe you find them pretty,  
Maybe you will not.
