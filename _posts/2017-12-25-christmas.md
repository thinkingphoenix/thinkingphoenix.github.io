title: Christmas
date: 2017-12-25 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [christmas, sorrow, pretense, pain, holiday, solitude]
---

Christmas is here.  
Hold your tears for tomorrow  
Act like you feel no sorrow.  

Christmas is here.  
Time to light up the dark rooms  
To smother the heart's black fumes.  

Christmas is here.  
Be a sport and let's celebrate  
And force this moment to feel ornate.
