---
title: Take Me Back
date: 2017-06-22 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [regret, past, pain, despair, memory]
---

Take me back  

Take me back  
To when I truly lived.  
Take me back  
To the edge of the cliff  

Where I stood  
A second before  
Wondering if I could  
Ever revoke  
The promises I made  
That one by one  
Stabbed in disdain.  

Take me back  
From the depth of the pit.  
Take me back  
Before the end of the trick.
