---
title: My Burning Soul
date: 2026-08-26 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [tag1, tag2]
---

I douse my head in water,  
It turns warm.  
This, is what you did to me.  

Hatred? No  
Affection? No  
All I fucking feel  
Is disgust.  
My distrust  
In humans is  
Reinforced by you.  
You, the fucking damsel in distress.  
For others' empathy, having keen interest.  
You are but a parasite;  
You feed on emotions.  
You talk of Aphrodite,  
Of Aether, of heavens.  
Ironic, when all you do  
Is break others just as much as yourself  
And move on to the next book on your shelf.  

I should have known better,  
God, do I regret my decision  
To make another human my muse.  
To keep someone within me,  
To ponder, pertain, peruse.  
I had fortified my heart,  
Because I couldn't risk more injuries.  
You let me open up,  
Convinced me it's safe to feel.  
When I missed your presence,  
You said you did too?  
I saw through your pretense;  
You act as if you've no clue.  
I survive and I will prevail,  
No matter what or who.  

Not you,  
Not fate,  
Not this world,  
Not love,  
Not hate,  
Shall fucking break me.  
Break, you do  
Under the reality  
That you can never accept.  
All you need is a hand that helps,  
Another tree you can plant and fell,  
Another sad story with tears to tell,  
To lure in another morsel of love  
And say you didn't ask for it.  
HAHAHAHA  
I see inside your fucking mind,  
The monster that hides behind  
Your breakdowns, tragedy and hurt,  
So it can lure in another human  
And bury their light with its dirt.  
I rip off pieces of my humanity  
From within my chest.  
Just to survive, to hide  
From filth like you.  
I trip off the edge of insanity,   
Falling beneath the Earth's crest.  
But escapes I'll devise  
Out of labyrinths like you.  

Pretty faces trigger my instincts.  
Now pretty words shall trigger them more.  
Fucking de-evolution is here,  
So I'll brace myself for the worse.  

This is the severance I make  
With my teeth on our bond.  
A bond? Or whatever the fuck that was.  
Like an ephemeral varnish drop on a canvas,  
Except nothing was painted,  
Nothing was preserved.  
Just my screams, faint,  
Into the void, dispersed.  
NO.  
I have to resurrect myself.  
I cannot stay helpless like this.  
I must correct myself.  
I cannot splay my own self like this.  

My blood turns darker.  
I'm quiet in this storm.  
With darkness I attain synchrony.  

I douse my head in water,  
It turns warm.  
This, is what you did to me.
