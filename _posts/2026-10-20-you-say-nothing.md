---
title: You Say Nothing
date: 2026-10-20 00:00:00 +0530
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [desperation, helplessness]
---

I kick against sorrow,  
Before it engulfs me.  
You say nothing  
Because it's what's easy.  

I punch on your walls,  
My knuckles break.  
You say nothing  
Because nothing's changed.  

I lay dead on your door,  
You won't open up.  
You say nothing  
Because you've had enough.  

I bargain for your love,  
You decline my offers.  
You say nothing  
Because I'm not the one, just another.  

I starve of your hunger,  
But that's the prophecy.  
You say nothing  
Because it worked, your alchemy.  

I curl into fetal position,  
For I can't stand this.  
You say nothing  
Because to you I'm outlandish.  

I try to let go  
And clutch onto you harder.  
You say nothing  
Because you've won this barter.  

I eradicate who I am,  
So you don't feel contempt.  
You say nothing  
Because I'm not what you dreamt.  

I growl what I go through,  
And it echoes and dies off.  
You say nothing  
Because your soul divides on.
