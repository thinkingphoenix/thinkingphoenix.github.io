---
title: Twelve
date: 2018-03-06 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [childhood, memories, growing-up, innocence, nostalgia, loss]
---

Incessantly I used to yell  
When Grandma had no stories to tell  
Sat there sulking by my bed  
I remember when I was twelve.  

Hurtling about in a tiny vest  
With nothing but cartoons in my head  
Feigning slumber, I hardly slept  
I remember when I was twelve.  

The summer's taste, far away went  
The milk has gone sour with stress  
One at a time, every dream fell  
It's been ages since I was twelve.
