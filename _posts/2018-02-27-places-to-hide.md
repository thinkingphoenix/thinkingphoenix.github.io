---
title: Places to Hide
date: 2018-02-27 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [despair, struggle, running-away, hopelessness, isolation, vulnerability]
---

When my dreams will be wiped off  
Like dust, before my eyes  
And my choking hopes deprived of  
Breath by violent tides,  
I'll run, but will be devoid of  
Places to hide.
