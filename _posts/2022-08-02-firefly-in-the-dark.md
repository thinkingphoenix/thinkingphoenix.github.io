---
title: Firefly in the Dark
date: 2022-08-02 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [hope, light, love, comfort, inspiration, warmth]
---

When it's dark and my shadow melts into it  
My feet freeze in panic; cold sweat all over  
A little dot of light, you illuminate my finger  
My tiny firefly, within me always you linger.  

Sometimes this is all I need, this hope,  
The immense strength to keep going  
A miniature sun in a cold winter,  
My tiny firefly, within me you always linger.  

Gentle as breeze yet tough as steel,  
You smile, glow in the bleak world.  
You're my muse, I'm your thinker  
My tiny firefly, within me you always linger.
