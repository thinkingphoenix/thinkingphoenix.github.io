---
title: Spring
date: 2018-04-17 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [nonet, romance, love, spring, nature, emotion]

When the breeze brings me your aroma  
And enough love for me to sink,  
I open up my heart's doors  
In verses, start to sing  
The music made  
In an eye's blink,  
When you brought  
Me this  
Spring.
