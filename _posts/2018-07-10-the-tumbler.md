---
title: The Tumbler
date: 2018-07-10 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [heartbreak, love, overflow, sadness, pain, loss]
---

The heart, a tumbler,  

Filled, filled, filled to the brim,  

Filled to the brim with love,  

With love, but none to drink,  

None to drink, so it overflowed,  

Overflowed till it was down in drain.  

Down in drain, of use to none again.
