---
title: Skinless
date: 2026-04-17 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [skinless, desire, love, romance]
---

My skull would love the way you hold it  
With your fingers stripped of skin.     
If there was no hide, no flesh,  
This is the intimacy I'd imagine.  
No make-up because there'd be  
No flaws haunting your consciousness  
And no scars that I usually carry.  
Just tasteful bodies as nature intended.   
Haven't you felt on some days  
That you lacked obsessive admiration?  
Maybe the same way I've felt  
That I was a mistaken creation?   
No envy, no resentment,  
Just a silent sigh, an unseen tear,   
An unfathomable yearning   
To have a human too near.   
Too near to your vulnerability   
Yet gently holding your fragile self.   
Translucent enough for you to   
See the heart and its blood vessels.  
And such has been my own desire;  
To not hurt but to make all bits   
Of a human feel loved. Not just  
Lips, but the tissues in it.   
To not just hold your hand but the   
Phalanges, metacarpals and carpals.   
For you to not just look into my eyes   
But through sockets, walk into my cortex cerebral.   
Practically impossible yet fascinating.   
Maybe because beauty is truly within.  
After all, your mellow breath comes   
From your lungs held without an inch  
To mine, and my back stands firm  
For you to hold and merge into  
Because of my vertebral column in it.  
After all, we're humans through and through.   
And we'd be grateful of the parts  
That make us the whole, the living things.  
If there were no lies, no pretense,  
This is the intimacy I'd imagine.
