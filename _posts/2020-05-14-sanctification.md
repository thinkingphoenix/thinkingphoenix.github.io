---
title: Sanctification
date: 2020-05-14 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [purification, fire, transformation, ashes, peace, closure]
---

Heap of cinder glows after   
Everything has been purified,  
By holy fire indifferent to none,  
That turns all into heat and light.  
Crimson no more, buried hachets  
Gray is the color of ashes.
