---
title: Amber
date: 2018-04-24 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [romance, eternity, devotion, love, timeless, warmth]
---

Hey, fiery amber... Have a favour to ask.  
Can you treasure a name, with a chunk of my beating heart?  
You'll defy time, and my love will be ever present,  
And be there for her to cherish; a myrrh of familiar essence.  
Just stay there, if she feels lonely.  
Melt in her arms, through you, she'll hold me.
