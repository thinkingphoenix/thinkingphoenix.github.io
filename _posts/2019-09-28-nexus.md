Late night at our terrace  
As three moons above watch us;  
Two full, and a third in Eclipse,  
There's a succulent bond... between our lips.  

Your visor glass fails to veil your eyes  
Enough for them to evade my gaze  
As your tresses dance to the rhythm of the breeze,  
As our power-cores pump to a synced beat,  
An old, country-style love song,  
From sometime after the second millennium  
Leaves its simmers in a tender drunkenness  
As our intensities strip our armors to a mess...  
Your hand wades through my hair,  
Metal fingers... cold yet subtle, warm...  
A human and a hydraulic arm embrace  
Me in a delicate hold; our personal space.  

Fluids and feelings flow;  
Our colors and curiosities mix up...  
As we explore each other  
And this newfound nexus.
