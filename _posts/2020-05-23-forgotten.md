---
title: Forgotten
date: 2020-05-23 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [nostalgia, memory, loss, passage-of-time, innocence, longing]
---

Pennies in my piggy bank  
My cartons of toy tanks,  
Old books I scribbled into  
Moments that we held on to,  
They lay fallen, down trodden,  
From memories, forgotten.
