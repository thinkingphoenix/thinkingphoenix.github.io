---
title: Mirror
date: 2019-10-15 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [dark, mystery, reflection, horror, abyss, identity]
---

Like everyday,  
I approached the mirror,  
Smiling the same way...  
Deigning my own ego  
Myself as well as  
Through the  
Flattened glassy soul  
We both spoke in sync  
When, suddenly, he won't budge  
And eyed me a sinister wink.  

A qualm creeping,  
Surging my gut.  
A chilly wave,  
Draining my blood.  
I heard a crack,  
A fine one in the reflection.  
Many more followed,  
Shards flew in random directions.  
"I'm sorry", yelled a voice.  
Within, I saw endless black  
Which sucked me into it...  
I've found yet, no way back.
