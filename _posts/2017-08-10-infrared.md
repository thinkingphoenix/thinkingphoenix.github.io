---
title: Infrared
date: 2017-08-10 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [pain, emptiness, solitude, hidden-emotions, struggle, despair]
---

What are you lookin' for in there?  
There's nothing within my heart.  
It's an empty shed  
Where once emotions took refuge  
And slept forever in thorn-beds.  
Ever since they never moved  
But left pain that's infrared  
That emits eerily every moment  
Yet you can't see it.  
It this is the way how life's spent  
At war with oneself, so be it.
