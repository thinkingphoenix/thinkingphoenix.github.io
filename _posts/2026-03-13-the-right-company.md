---
title: The Right Company
date: 2026-03-13 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [company, kinship, bonding]
---

Come here, have a seat.    
There's no alcohol or weed,    
But something better for you.  
I have conversations that never    
Made it out of your mind.  
Discussions we're constantly  
Searching for, but never find.  
The long letters you've carved   
Beneath your skin,    
Read them out to me.  
Let it remind you that  
We too are humans, despite  
The intensity of how we feel.  
I'd tell you about the poems  
I wish I had burnt.  
You tell me about the silences  
That grew so loud they hurt.  
My eyes can only see so much.  
I will adore the charm within you.  
Show me where it hurts,  
I'll lend you the bandages I use.  
It's okay to sink.  
To have a heavy heart.  
Because heavy weights build strong shoulders.  
Because I too have a soul that's smouldered,  
Slowly suffocated :  sometimes by others,  
Sometimes at my own hands.  
Hence, I understand.  
It's tough out here.  
So weep, scream, bawl.  
I'll join to witness it all.
