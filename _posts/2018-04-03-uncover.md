---
title: Uncover
date: 2018-04-03 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [humor, playful, love, sweet, sensory, twist]
---

My fingers over you, want to play  

Your tough, dark demeanour, ripped away  

I see your uncoloured, naive self  

Your ambrosial body, sweet and svelte  

I lick what's within  

Devour what's dark.  

Oh my darling Oreo,  

You're a piece of my heart.
