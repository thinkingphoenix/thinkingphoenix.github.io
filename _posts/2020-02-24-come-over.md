---
title: Come Over
date: 2020-02-24 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [romance, longing, intimacy, passion, desire, first-time]
---

Tonight, you're home alone...  
I wish I could come over.  

Knock! Knock! On your door,  
I prefer it over ringing bells.  
You unlatch it, curious  
Ecstatic to see me, I can tell.  

No greetings or good-evenings;  
Our eyes talk in their own lingos.  
Wild passions wait no longer  
As we cuddle into one, like flamingos.  

Resting a hand by your waist,  
And the other stroking your hair  
Like my guitar strings, I heave  
A heavy breath, unzipping the jacket you wear.  

Pecking my way through your neck,  
I reach my destination, your lips.  
All mischief breaks loose  
As I make up for all we missed.  

I take off your glasses,  
My thumb caressing your eyeline.  
There's a tiny tear of joy  
In mine, for it's my first time.  

You push me to the wall,  
Caging me between your arms.  
I grow shy, but retaliate,  
Kissing you hard, you're my lucky charm.  

I unleash the artist in you,  
As you breath into me, colors  
Holding each second, slower...  

Tonight, you're home alone...  
I wish I could come over.
