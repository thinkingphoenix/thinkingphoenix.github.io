---
title: Blank Pages
date: 2018-01-30 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [emptiness, solitude, writing, dark-light, despair, existence]
---

Blank pages  
Glare through my flesh  
Nothingness creeps  
In every breath, a mess  
This life seems  
Cursed and blessed  
By words that stream  
Inked in hollowness  
What do I dream?  
Lemme guess.  
Of dark light beams  
More or less.
