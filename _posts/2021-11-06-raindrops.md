---
title: Raindrops
date: 2021-11-06 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [romance, longing, happiness, memories, rain, warmth]
---

Just at the doorstep of sleep  
I'm flooded with feelings  
Not just knee-deep, I'm knocked off my feet  
You are all over this place,  
Like a strewn collection of cassettes and tapes;  
In this place which my soul lives in  
And where now your colors fill in.  
I'm kindled with embers of life  
My lips feel how smiling is like.  
I've nothing to win or lose,  
Memories are my treasure.  
Because true happiness within  
Comes from things we can't measure.  

I type away as raindrops knock,  
My friends asleep knowing not of  
My eyes yet awake  
My eyes that forever await,  
For the next glance I steal of her,  
What new stories our fates offer.
