---
title: Sow/Reap
date: 2018-01-16 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [heartbreak, irony, disillusionment, grief, pain, betrayal]
---

What you sow is what you reap?  
(Who the fuck said that, please?)  

I sowed smiles  
Then why do I weep?  
I sowed love  
Got hatred to keep.  
I sowed joy  
And wept on a Christmas Eve.  
I sowed passion  
Now lay among the fall's leaves.  
I sowed happiness  
Now my meals are garnished by grief.  
...and what you sow is what you reap.
