---
title: Fireflies and Stars
date: 2019-09-09 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [nature, stars, fireflies, beauty, hope, memories]
---

Twinkling in the night  
In the darkness they shine  
Giving people light  
Making sure they're feeling fine  
One is called a star  
Which is very far  
The other is a firefly  
A tiny life which can fly  
The difference between a firefly  
And a star,  
Is that the firefly can die  
While the star watches from afar.  

Somewhere frozen in time,  
These scintillations oh so divine  
Pour poetry into rhymes;  
One miles away, one close by, flyin'.  
One is called a star  
Which is very far  
The other is a firefly  
A tiny life which can fly.  
What's common in a firefly  
And a star,  
Is that they make us smile  
Or weep, bracing memories from past.
