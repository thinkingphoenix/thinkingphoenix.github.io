---
title: Dream It Big
date: 2017-08-24 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [reality, darkness, despair, ambition]
---

Dream it big  

Okay now close your eyes together  
Go, make a wish...  
Oh, but remember...  
Not to dream it big  
In this world that eats you raw  
Imperfect, but points at your flaw  
Make boundaries when you thinkin'  
On your imagination, put limits  
But...  
Uh-oh. Look what you've done  
You actually nurtured hopes  
Now hang by a rope  
Round your neck, an inch thick.  
I told you, fucker,  
When you made the wish  
Not to dream it big.
