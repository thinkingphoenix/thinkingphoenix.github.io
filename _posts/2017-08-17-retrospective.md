title: Retrospective
date: 2017-08-17 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [self-reflection, realization, solitude, honesty, pain, struggle]
---

A friend read my diary today.  
"You write nice", he says,  
"But all of this was your own mistake  
And never was a game of fate."  
My head nods  
Lost in thought  
And finally did I conclude  
Why I was so confused  
About why's everything so screwed up  
Why I set out searching for new drugs  
It was me behind my own murder  
But now I brace myself and don't give a fuck  
About what the mundane world thinks of me  
As I sit below the old Peepal tree  
All I've got is me for company  
'Cause I fight with me first  
And then come the enemies.
