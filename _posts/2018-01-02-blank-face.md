---
title: Blank Face
date: 2018-01-02 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [darkness, pain, memory, phantom, solitude, despair]
---

Venom wanders within my veins  
As I whisper viewing my night visions  
Vignettes of that voice and visage  
The voice vociferates in the walls of the person  
That once vivaciously called my name  
Poison spreads; I'm reality-smitten  
Soon enough a farewell to be made  
The hauntings and hallucinations are the very reason  
The beings like me carry a blank-face.
