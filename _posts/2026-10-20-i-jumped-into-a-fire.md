---
title: I Jumped Into a Fire
date: 2026-10-20 00:00:00 +0530
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [aftermath, hurt, separation]
---

I jumped into a fire.   
Now I'm all burnt.   

I jumped into a fire,  
The fire that thawed my ice.  
The fire within your eyes  
That in darkness, gave me light.  

I jumped into a fire,  
The fire that was your passion.  
The fire, that now is for arson,  
That incinerates, that never pardons.    

I jumped into a fire,  
The fire that kept me safe.  
The fire, that now sets me ablaze,  
And leaves me in tears of rage.  

I jumped into a fire,  
The fire that lit my path.  
The fire, now all soot, embers and ash    
Of my beloved that I can't bring back.

I jumped into a fire,  
Thinking it was a trial.  
Why have I not realised 
That it's just my heart, suicidal?  

I jumped into a fire,  
I'm no longer human, I've turned.  
Into what? I don't know.  
I jumped into a fire, 
And now I'm all burnt.
