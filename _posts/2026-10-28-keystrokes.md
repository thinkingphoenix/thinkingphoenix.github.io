My keystrokes know me the best.   
From anyone else, I'm the fool to expect.   
My head hurts  
And I resist the urge  
To utter a word to you,  
Lest I hear your haughty, disdainfull voice.  
Your mockery of me in which you revel, rejoice.  

I saw you as my Queen,  
You saw me as a pauper.  
So go on, fill your palace with your laughter.  
Go on, for I will write it all down, I'm the drafter.  

I keep typing. I keep talking  
To no one. Certainly not you.  
(As if you would from within your shell)  
(I brought this upon myself)  
For I gambled, pawned my heart  
And oh so easily lost you.  

Just random numbers of fate  
That stomp over our wishful thinking of destiny.  
Just another loser who tried to create  
A heaven, a home in high air density.  
A word. A word is all you had to say.  
But you chose to rather push me into the gray.  

My keystrokes know me the best.  
For anyone else, none of me is left.  
My head hurts  
And I stop my neck that turns  
To see if you're alright.  
But I recall what happens to me isn't past your vanity.  

You can villainize me.  
I can villainize you just as much.  
You can run, hide,  
Cling to whatever lets you sleep at night.  
My mind's fucked anyway.  
You might as well enjoy this sight.  

My keystrokes hold me from falling.  
My keystrokes never neglect.  
To bystanders, I'm the madman  
With letters twisting inside his head.  
My keystrokes know me the best.  

All that glitters may just be garbage.  
All of it just one sick joke.  
All of what we perceive as love,  
Maybe just charlatans well-versed in its language.  

But then, the fuck does it matter anyway?  
Why should I endure, endure and give leeways?  
What looks is what sells,  
I should have known that well.  

And a marketplace isn't where I belong.  
I belong here, with my words,  
With keystrokes that I mostly don't get wrong.   

My tears left me a while ago.  
I have nothing to mourn with.  
I'm used to this shock now,  
I let my words talk now.  

For I issue a  cease and desist.  
For nothing I hold dear can exist.  
There is nothing to sow, nothing to harvest  
From anyone else, I'm a fool to expect.  
My keystrokes know me the best.
