---
title: Behind the Canvas 
date: 2026-09-24 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [introspection, existential dread, art]
---
I hide  
Behind the canvas.  
The width of the board,  
The threshold,  
The liminal space it holds,  
Is not to be crossed.  

I draw and color,  
Or leave it grayscale.  
I show it to others,  
They admire and appreciate.  
They don't and they should not  
Peek beyond the paper, within me.  
They don't and should not  
Peel off the art I wear gracefully.  
Unsightly,  
The creature underneath   
Might become their nightmare.  
Unsightly,  
How on intangible beauty I feed  
And regurgitate art in despair.  

I have broken  
Crayons,  
Pencils,  
Paint bottles,  
Oil pastels,  
Ink vials.  
I am broken.  
Aeons    
Of guilt,    
Vain battles,  
Destroyed angels,  
Infinite trials. 

Crimson,  
Scarlet,  
Vermilion,  
Sanguine;  
I am too familiar with red.  
As it gushes through me,  
Oozes out of me for none to see. 

Nyctophile,  
The serene nights are my heaven.  
Sun scorches me,  
Leaving me vulnerable, deadened.  

I survive,  
Rummaging for   
Surfaces to draw on,  
Since I can't contain it all,  
I must scratch my claws on  
Something that doesn't disintegrate  
Unlike me.  

So, ensconced in my chamber,  
I sketch lines  
Or write stanzas.  
I hide  
Behind the canvas.
