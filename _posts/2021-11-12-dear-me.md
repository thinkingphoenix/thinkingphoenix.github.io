---
title: Dear Me
date: 2021-11-12 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [self-love, hope, comfort, resilience, healing, mental-health]
---

Cheer up now, dear me.  
Life's too short, you hear me?  

Yesterday, you had a nightmare  
Being terrified does not mean you're weak.  
Tomorrow, let's put a dream-catcher  
By the window to watch over your sleep.  

Cheer up now, dear me.  
Life's too short, you hear me?  

Yesterday, you felt cornered  
Your room suffocating you like a cage.  
Tomorrow, let's dial up numbers  
Of friends you've not seen for days.  

Cheer up now, dear me.  
Life's too short, you hear me?  

Yesterday, you passed by the mirror  
And felt insecure once again.  
Tomorrow, remember that you're you  
And no two existences are supposed to be same.  

Cheer up now, dear me.  
Life's too short, you hear me?  

Yesterday, you were empty  
From the love you give but don't receive.  
Tomorrow, you must realise  
It's just fate, whom you can or can't keep.  

Cheer up now, dear me.  
Life's too short, you hear me?  

Yesterday, you smoked  
Asking the lighter, why so terrible life felt.  
Tomorrow you'll have known  
It's far easier without hopes and regrets.  

Go rest now, dear me.  
Life's too short, you hear me?
