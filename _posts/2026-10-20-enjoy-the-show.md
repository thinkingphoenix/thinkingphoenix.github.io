title: Enjoy the Show!
date: 2026-10-20 00:00:00 +0530
categories: [Poetry]

Ladies and Gentlemen!  
I present to you  
What you may not comprehend!  
I will show you how a man breaks  
And dances still to the music!  
I call upon one and all   
To wonder and view this :  

Watch him beg for mercy, (hahahaha)  
To question if there's anything he's worthy  
Of. Not love certainly! (hahahaha)  
Watch his desperate attempts to speak  
While he's from the inside, weak.  
See him mechanically move  
And dance to the beat!  

Isn't it hilarious!?   
These creatures I've handpicked  
From places dark, derelict (gasp)  
But not to worry!  
He's harmless.  
He harms none  
But his own self! (hahahaha)  

Such a fool, see him weep  
For wounds invisibly deep.  
His existence is for bemusement,   
He makes humor out of torment!  

Oh wait, his insides are showing.  
Don't worry, he'll soon be up and moving.  
What? Why is he lying motionless?  
*Agh, bring a fresh one tomorrow, nonetheless.*
