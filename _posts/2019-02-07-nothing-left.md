---
title: Nothing Left
date: 2019-02-07 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [heartbreak, betrayal, sorrow, darkness, loss, hindi]
---

I know there've been nights  
When you've bitterly wept,  
Clutching between your teeth  
Secrets no one kept.  
Colourlessly bleed your eyes  
The nights when you never slept.  
Rummaging for a ray of comfort  
Of which you've always been bereft,  
You ended up in wrong hearts  
Holding but trickery and theft  
Of all you've ever been  
Degrading you, step by step.  
By the time you realise  
It gets late; there's nothing left.
