---
title: Eyesore
date: 2023-04-16 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [truth, alienation, confrontation, dark, flaw, illusion]
---

I am the scream  
You don't want to shout aloud.  
I am the problem  
You don't want to talk about.  

Your world is vivid blooms;  
Withered flowers left to decay.  
I'm alive amid all words  
You decide not to say.  

I'm the ruined fortress  
In your concrete circus city.  
You think you can heal—  
I'm a specimen of your inability.  

I'm the unexpected flaw  
In your perfect design.  
I break your laws  
By speaking my mind.  

Your lives are naïve negligence,  
Shells of joy easily shattered.  
You let slide signs of sorrows,  
Sheltered beneath your skin, scattered.  

I'm the unhappy ending  
To your fabricated fairy tales.  
I unmask your synthetic smiles  
To reveal your faces pale.
