---
title: The Ugly Side of Love
date: 2023-04-03 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [cynicism, heartbreak, modern-love, grief, honesty]
---

You might understand  
My hatred for this world  
Teeming with hypocritical romantics  
Who've over ages, hurled  
Lies shaped like poetry,  
At us. If matches are made above  
Then why have I, oh Lord  
Seen only the ugly side of love?  

I live in this hollow crowd  
Of apathetic humans  
Pretending to be empathetic.  
Soulmates replaceable with new ones.  
I see through kindness now—  
The sycophancy I can judge.  
My eyes are so adept as I've  
Seen only the ugly side of love.  

We're in that decadent age  
Where Romeo is just an "option",  
As thousands wait for their luck  
For Juliet's love is for auction.  
The casual culture curtails  
Chances to be pure enough  
To form real bonds, they've just  
Been only the ugly side of love.
