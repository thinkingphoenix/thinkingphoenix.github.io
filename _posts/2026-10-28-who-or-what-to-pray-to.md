---
title: Who/What to Pray To?
date: 2026-10-28 09:28:07 +0530
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [despair, atheism, doom]
---

Does praying make us feel lighter?  
If so, I would pray throughout my breakdown.  
But who/what do I pray to? I have no answer.  
For the Gods must be amused at this clown.  

How else do I explain what happens to me?  
That I build myself for an apocalypse,  
Yet I end up downtrodden, defeated so easily.  
I've battened down the hatches,  
And yet can't stop myself from sinking.  
My cries swallowed by the sea,  
This is what I deserve, for I've defied my instincts.  

Does praying reverse your injury?  
If so, I would pray till my heart from nothing, regrows.  
But who/what do I pray to? I have no answer.  
For I'm convinced, there is no sanctuary for my woes.  

How else do I explain this catastrophe?  
Every passing second, I age by an year.  
I muster strength, only to be annihilated by my fears.  
Your thoughts that melted my heart, now burn my skin.  
What am I repenting for? How heavy have been my sins?  
I understand not, for I am a vile creature  
Devoid of divinity, devoid of devotion.  
Glass may shatter from my silent shrieks  
At any kirk, at my sight an altar may creak.  
For that's how much the Gods hate me.  

So, does praying provide me redemption?  
If so, I would pray until this play is over.  
But who/what do I pray to? I have no answer.  
So like the ghost I am, in this plane I hover.  

You who read this  
Quietly,  
With your rehearsed  
Apathy,  
Must be in peace.  
I envy your ability  
To shove aside your grief.  
That is, if you have any.  
That is, if it wasn't all felony.  

I cannot pray anymore.  
We live in a world of sacrilege.  
I will continue to exist,  
The Gods may continue to hate.  
I am alive and I will take that privilege.
