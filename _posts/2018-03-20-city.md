---
title: City
date: 2018-03-20 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [city, dreams, struggle, solitude, existential, passage-of-time]
---

A thousand dreams come  
A thousand dreams stay  
A thousand dreams leave  
This city everyday.  

How much of my soul  
Am I leaving behind  
In the maze of lanes?  

How much of my sins  
Have I failed to shrive  
In the kirk of saints?  

A thousand leaves rustle  
A thousand leaves decay  
A thousand dreams leave  
This city, everyday.  

How long will I cope  
Eating spiteful flaks  
On my open flanks?  

How far will I run  
In the dead of night  
With a burnt-out lamp?  

A thousand lights glisten  
A thousand lights fade  
A thousand dreams leave  
This city, everyday.
