---
title: Swings
date: 2017-07-06 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [nostalgia, childhood, memories, solitude, passage-of-time, grief]
---

Two lonely swings sway  
Bored on sunny days  
In wait for someone  
To become a pendulum.  

One empty swing here  
The next spilled with tears  
Still with heaviness  
Of some heart's sediments.  

Two swinging friends then  
Stories sharing often  
Molten ice cream stains  
Bits of tired paper planes.  

Two lost memories now  
They both left the town  
The swings sway, now old  
And got me this story told.
