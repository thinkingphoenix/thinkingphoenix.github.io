---
title: Bridge
date: 2023-04-21 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [hope, connection, support, resilience, love, companionship]
---

I have a verse  
Made of unspoken words.  
I string them together  
And extend to your heart.  

Hold on to it  
When life gets hard  
And you need a reason to go on.  

To fight and dare being yourself  
In this atmosphere of lies,  
Pretence and deception  
Of people's intentions so vile.  

Rise with me and  
I'll bleed for you  
Far it may seem but  
We'll find what's true.
