---
title: Grandma's Jewel Box
date: 2019-09-16 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [memories, nostalgia, aging, loneliness, collaboration, heirloom]
---

Nobody's home, just as always;  
One of the usual evenings  
Old grandma sighs on her rocking chair,  
With the familiar lonely feeling.  

Clutching within her weathered hands,  
An old casket, still younger than her,  
She wipes from it, a day's worth of dust.  
Opens it again, as though seeking an answer.  

Gold ornaments from her golden youth  
That withstood the test of time;  
Strong as herself, saw her man leave,  
And a child with a far, settled life.  

***Jade:***  
*Her aged hand passes over them.*  
*Their smooth faces begin to warm,*  
*Saying though her world was now lonely;*  
*From unwritten letters, unmade calls,*  

*Unmade visits from distant friends,*  
*Unsent invites from relatives,*  
*Her jewelry were talismans*   
*That stored all of her fond memories.*  

*Her life had known some happy times,*  
*Her jewelry had known them too.*  
*She remembers them all and sighs,*  
*Smiling in her lonely, quiet room.*
