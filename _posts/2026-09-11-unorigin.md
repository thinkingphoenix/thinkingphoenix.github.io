---
title: UnOrigin
date: 2026-09-11 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [existential dread, agony, internal conflict]
---

This fucking pit of hell  
Is where I have originated.  
I have burnt myself,  
I have blamed myself,  
I have hurt myself,  
I have maimed myself.  
My life supports crumble  
Under the weight of metaphysical ailments.  
Who am I apologizing to?  
Mother? Brother? Family? Friends?  
I owe them all and I  
Try and try  
To hold my reality together  
With my withering hands,  
In my eternal endeavour.  
This torment began...  
The fuck...? When did it begin?  
I cannot recall.  
It matters fuckall.  
Maybe when perception sunk its teeth into me.  
When the world felt colder than it seemed to be.  
Since long have I  
Been running.  
Running into fires    
To feel warm,  
To feel something even if I burned.  
Is it worth all I have turned  
Into?  
Is this incurable despondence   
A price to pay for art?  
Why God (if you exist)  
Must I suffer? Have I done wrong?  
I've never intended to hurt,  
Somehow I have been scorned.  
I've never intended hatred  
And you make me hate until it's all I am.  
If your hands can fix me,  
Wade your fingers into my chest  
Like a hot knife on butter.  
I will not a word utter.  
Make it excruciating if it's fun for you  
But make it feel better   
Than this fucking empty shell  
I live in, so distastefully culminated.  
Than this fucking pit of hell  
Where I have originated.
