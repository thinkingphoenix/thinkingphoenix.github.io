---
title: Flood
date: 2020-09-13 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [anxiety, pain, drowning, despair, darkness, struggle]
---

Eyes torn awake  
I stare at the ceiling,  
As if it knew answers  
To why I'm feeling  
Like this very air in my lungs  
Is turning heavy, and much heavier,  
Until it deliquesces and dominates  
My resolve to respire. Sudden fear  
Engulfs my insides,  
Like a malignant flood  
Invisible to the crowd.  
I flail and fight as I drown,  
Kicking at the merciless water.  
Cold hands, cold feet,  
Still heart, dead beats.  
I relinquish the hands of hope,  
Being struck by abrupt, eerie peace  
Of being a burden no more,  
I give in to sweet, submerged sleep.
