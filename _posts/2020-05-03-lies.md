---
title: Lies
date: 2020-05-03 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [betrayal, heartbreak, lies, pain, grief, darkness, struggle]
---

They're seeping into my skin  
Weathering me from within  
Everything I chose to believe  
Is now an endless void, pitch black  
In my ruins,  emotions erode...  
Only a million lies remain, intact.  

They were fed into me, gradually...  
Like weak venom with love, casually  
Until they dominated my veins  
And I wept under their impact.  
Like aged metal, I completely corrode...  
Only a million lies remain, intact.  

The morning rays sting my eyes.  
As I lay here, motionless, vandalized  
By what all I ever held to my chest,  
By what all that's never coming back.  
My tears are now a cyphered code...  
Only a million lies remain, intact.  

The damage is beyond what I can surmise,  
B1roken once, twice, thrice, many times,  
Corpse of my feelings disfigured,  
What remains is my name on the dog-tag.  
It all ends with this ordealistic ode...  
Only a million lies remain, intact.
