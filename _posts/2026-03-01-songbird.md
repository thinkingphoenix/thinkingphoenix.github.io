---
title: Songbird
date: 2026-03-01 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [grit, retrospection, transition]
---

I'm a songbird that sings,  
I'm only heard, not seen.  
I'm admired,  
But haven't been  
A creature desired.  
Now save your sympathies—  
I need none of it.  
I'm not the antique  
Of your circus—  
I'm a misfit.  

I'm a songbird that used to sing  
Charming tunes hitherto.  
Charm, which withered  
Into black and blue,  
Forcefully reversed.  
Now my voice exists to  
Soothe the downtrodden.  
My pain persists to  
Haunt the human and  
Lift the fallen.  

So, go chase the   
Damned pretty birds.  
Let me embrace my  
Music with harsh words.  
I claim pleasure   
In all that hurts and stings.  
I am the fucking songbird  
That continues to sing.
