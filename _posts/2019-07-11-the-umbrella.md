---
title: The Umbrella
date: 2019-07-11 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [rain, nature, reflection, life, carefree, solitude]
---

Drip, drip, drip, hit the rain  
 roads, the streets  
The cars on four wheels.  
The people, struggling in glued jeans,  
All scurried on slippery feet.  

I hummed my favourite tunes  
And thankfully grinned at the sky.  
My tshirt soaked, I much looked like  
An insane bloke, to the passers-by  
Who rushed in rain  
That dampened the feel  
Of the whole terrain.  

My, you could've come in handy...  
The little umbrella that I never carried.
