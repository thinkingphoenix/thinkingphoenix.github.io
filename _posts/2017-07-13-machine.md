---
title: Machine
date: 2017-07-13 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [artist, struggle, expectations, pain, despair, dreams]
---

I wish to be a machine  
listen to orders see no dreams.  
It's a curse  
to be an artist  
I can never see faith in my moms eyes  
All my words  
Akin to garbage;  
I wish I could be someone's pride.  

I run from reality  
Carrying hopes that don't exist.  
It's my alacrity  
Trying to speak with sewed lips.  

Why can't I be normal?  
Like the nerdy guy living next door.  
Why am I so awful?  
That I can't make it to the scholarship score?  

Stop scribbling and land up in a good job  
Work 9 to 5, settle down and get lost  
People see art; they praise  
And yet the creator's life is dead waste.  

We get recognised  
When our remains decay  
So I wish to be a machine and not break the chain.
