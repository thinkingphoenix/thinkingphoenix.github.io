---
title: Hide And Seek
date: 2020-01-11 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [dark, thriller, suspense, horror, fear, hide-and-seek]
---

Drip, drip, drip...  
Red on the floor.  
Knock, knock, knock!  
Who's at the door?  
On toe tips  
Find somewhere to hide.  
Click, click, click...  
Turn off all the lights.  
Hold your breath,  
Or she'll hear you.  
Brace yourself,  
For she's near you.  
Beep, beep, beep...!  
Your cellphone rings.  
Do, re, mi...  
She starts to sing.  
"I found you now!",  
She's on her knees.  
You're in her arms.  
Now rest in peace.
