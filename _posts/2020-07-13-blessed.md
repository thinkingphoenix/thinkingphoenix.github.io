---
title: Blessed
date: 2020-07-13 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [gratitude, love, tranquility, nonet, peace, devotion]
---

Late nights make me think often about  
What all's mine, though I don't deserve.  
To my heart she descends down,  
She is forever heard,  
Like the song of dawn,  
Chirped by the birds  
In their nests.  
I feel  
Blessed.
