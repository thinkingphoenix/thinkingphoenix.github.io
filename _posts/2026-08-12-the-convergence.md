---
title: The Convergence
date: 2026-08-12 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [convergence, connection, poetry, soul, reflection, hindi]
---

The city glows at night. 
Neon sign boards, displays desperate to sell,  
Humans with malice within, stories they never tell.  
Disconnected from this dimension,  
My being is another trigger in the automation.  

The city blooms in the morning (so do you).  
Bustling alleyways, dogs and cats stretching   
In this air so heavy, where you still find life dwelling.  
Disconnected from this dimension,  
Your being quietly craves for an ascension.  

And we so poetically converge.  
Maybe because we both adore poetry, because we weep words?  
Like a tryst between dawn and dusk, on the horizon’s verge,  
Where for a brief moment, pain gives us leeway; it stops to hurt.  

Your soul is ancient.   
Your fascination : Gods, Angels and Demons.  
My soul, awaiting apocalypses,  
Of machinations : info wars, hacktools and daemons.   

You would see history in me.  
I would see the future in you.  
You punish yourself for the past.  
I cherish you for your fortitude.  

I stand still and observe the Moon  
At nights, with towers and scrapers underneath,  
Like big boxes of humans, equipment, struggles and subdued sleep.  
I stand still and then the time reads noon.  
What I wish to tell you, I murmur in my dreams.  
By the evening, the stray thoughts arrange themselves on my screen.   

Because we so poetically converge.  
What I never knew was oppressed, you have liberated.  
Then why, tell me, do your tears fall isolated?  
Give me my share, drench in them will I for you, elated.
