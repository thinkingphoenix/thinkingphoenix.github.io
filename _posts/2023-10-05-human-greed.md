---
title: Human Greed
date: 2023-10-05 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [humans, finite, greed, life, death]
---
For what are we but mere creatures of flesh and bone?  
We cannot take to grave what we think we own.  
Vivid springs fade into monochrome as eyes close.  
So will the bodies run cold that our arms hold.  
Remnants of the scent from last night in the bed  
Will be drenched in sorrow of the one behind left.  
As existence gets reduced to memories,  
Limited to human lives of less than a century,  
Here we are yet wishing for something eternal,  
Something straight taken from God's journal,  
While unbeknownst of what little it takes to end  
Everything in the world we do or don't comprehend.
