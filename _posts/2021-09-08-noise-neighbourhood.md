---
title: Noise/Neighbourhood
date: 2021-09-08 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [nostalgia, memory, war, silence, neighborhood, loss]
---

Buzz, buzz, buzz!  
The housefly whizzes by  
In circles, stealing a sugar-smidgen.  

Thwack, thwack, thwack!  
The lady washing, beats  
Life out of the clothes of her husband.  

Psst, psst, psst!  
Calls a young girl, her friends  
To join in gossips from her school event.  

Grrang, grrang, grrang!  
Groans the brute bulldozer  
Scooping out chunks of Earth with worms in them.  

Drukk, drukkk, drukk!  
My aunt's sewing machine  
Hums as she stitches in place, a skirt's hem.  

Crick, crick, crick!  
Crickets love commotion.  
Screaming, they scatter as rain drops descend.  

These noises now resonante in my ears  
As I return home after several years.  

Disturbing silence now everywhere roams;  
My neighborhood's now ruins of a warzone.
