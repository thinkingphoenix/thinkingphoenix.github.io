This is a guide for you.  
You, who are yet to be here.  

First, put down your  
Sword and shield :  
This is safe space.  
Tell me how you feel,  
Unchain yourself  
As I emerge from   
My grave for you,  
(I've craved for you)  
Our fierce forms  
Reduced to just  
Spirits in love.  
Unbutton my solitude  
As I unveil your scars.  
This dark, quiet night  
With you is what I choose  
Over their moon and stars.  
Gently examine my  
Heartbeat. Run your  
Fingers on my chest.  
Caress me some more.  
By now my body shows  
Signs of life, right?  
Now try locking your  
Eyes again with mine.  
Do you see my soul  
Calling for you?  
Do you feel my breath  
Heavily rising for you?  
If yes, then hold me closer.  
In my ear, whisper  
My name. I am your  
Warm winter.  
Don't keep me waiting.  
Swiftly close the gap  
Between our lips.  
Take them in your grasp :  
Moist, bite, nibble  
Them. Greet my tongue  
With yours, fiddle  
As much as you want.  
Because we're infinite,  
Albeit in human bodies   
With a fixed span of life.  
Can it stop us from  
Loving like we're dying next?  
No.  
So, infuse yourself  
Into me as I sink  
Into all you are.  
Connect all our links.  

I'll keep my heart alive for you,  
I'll hold on to hope for you, dear.     
This is—take note—a guide for you.  
You, who are yet to be here.
