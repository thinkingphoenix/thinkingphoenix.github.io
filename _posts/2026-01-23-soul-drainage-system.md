---
title: Soul Drainage System
date: 2026-01-23 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [soul, self-sabotage, rot, retrospection]
---

Drop by drop it drains away,  
My very soul which I let others feed on,  
Dangling on my knees while I let them lean on.  
Each word I hear, a sliver of hope  
Of being understood, to be seen  
Beneath my flesh, how life has been.  
Drop by drop they drink away,  
While I let them, oblivious of what remains of me.  
Unaware of the wound opening wider, starting to bleed.  
And the blood empties, it starts to grey out,  
Its death reflecting in my eyes  
As it tries to find a way out.  
I let it decompose within,  
And enslave it to let me function.  
And yet drop by drop it flows away  
As invisible tears of profound sorrow,  
While I force myself to face tomorrow.
