---
title: Fool's Gold
date: 2020-04-01 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [deception, disillusionment, betrayal, caution, heartbreak, truth]
---

Liars everywhere,  
Bragging about  
Feelings they don't have.  

Luring passers-by  
With folktales  
Into a shiny trap.  

Never will I again  
Fall for words that don't hold.  
Never will I again  
Fall for gold, fool's gold.
