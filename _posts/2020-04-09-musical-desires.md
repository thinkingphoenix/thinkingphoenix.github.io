---
title: Musical Desires
date: 2020-04-09 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [romance, passion, intimacy, music, petrichor, desire]
---

Left open a window, we sat there  
With petrichors from evening rain, in air  
She, facing me with her guitar  
Me, listening with a thumping heart  
Her hair is loose, a perfect mess  
I hum the song with her, playing with a tress  
She's distracted, misses a note  
In a flash, I draw her close  
Picking a lily, from her desk  
Brushing it by her bare neck  
I strip off her guitar, it's my turn  
To make music, my desires burn  
As she falls silent; breaths heavy,  
Blow off the candles she lets me.  
The rain's still pouring, the night's still young.  
And our chords of passion, we start to strum.  
She reflects mischief, her eyes lit  
As our chapters unfold, unabridged.  
We're in key with the song of the rain,  
Happily aside from the world mundane.
