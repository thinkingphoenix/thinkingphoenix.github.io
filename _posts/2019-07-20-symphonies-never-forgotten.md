---
title: Symphonies Never Forgotten
date: 2019-07-20 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [tribute, chester-bennington, linkin-park, memories, grief, healing]
---

Dear seraphs up above, from heaven's end  
Descend, oh descend upon the sepulchre of my friend  
Let the parted soul feel  
The symphonies never forgotten.  

Immortal memories from moments evanescent  
In black skies, the only crescent  
A shimmer on everything darkened,  
Like an afterlife, ethereal,  
The symphonies never forgotten.  

---

*In the loving memory of Chester Bennington. Thank you for shaping my life, saving me from my worst nights of pain, making me feel that I was not alone. May you always be in peace on the other side. I miss you.*
