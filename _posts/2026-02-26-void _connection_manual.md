title: Void _Connection_Manual
date: 2026-02-26 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [void, connection, introspection, explanation]
---
This void is my home.  
Not cold, not empty,  
But full of words  
That I shove into my poetry.  
They echo, within my walls  
And return to me, ever loyal  
Cutting through all that's false.   
Connections I have many.   
I'm blessed by those   
Who stand for me.   

And yet this void is my home,  
Burning the feet   
Of those trying to enter  
By lies and deceit.  

But I shall keep these  
Doors open, just in case  
A connection meant for me  
Appears and needs me  
To initiate the sequence,  
To demolish these walls,  
To break free of this world  
And its pretence.  
I keep losing the structure  
Of how my thoughts   
Should be put to words,  
Polished by chrome.  
Yet this void is my home,    
Not harsh, not cruel,  
But full of my dualities  
That each minute duel.
