---
title: Place to Weep
date: 2018-01-09 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [solitude, pain, sorrow, crying, night, despair]
---

When my head throbs so much so  
And my heart hurts much so more  
And my throat sports a nasty sore  
And my soul oozes like blood from a gore  

When pain and hurt like raindrops seep  
I find myself some place to weep.  

When no one's there to call me weak  
And the noisy night is all that speaks  
With the passive wind when the old door creaks  
Mocking my life that is oh so bleak  

When sorrowed joys like neighbours peep  
I find myself some place to weep.
