---
title: The Raven's Call
date: 2019-07-11 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [dark, mystery, reflection, solitude, death, mortality]
---

When it's time  
For me to go,  
I'll look back  
At all I owned,  
Words sans meanings,  
Birds with cropped wings,  
A house that wasn't home,  
Concerns that no one showed,  
Windows that stayed locked,  
Whispers that swayed, lost.  

I'll look back, in the end  
When cold fear  
Draws down the pall.  
I'll look back, and pretend  
Not to hear  
The raven's call.
