title: Between the Lines
date: 2021-04-03 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]

We might live 60 or 70 years  
In sleepless cities or countryside  
Time flies, shifting gears  
Feels like we just met last night  

My eyes on you, it went raining in my heart  
And all I want  
Is to hold you, get close to you, go home with you, turn old with you  

But there's still time...  
'Cause I just can't read your mind  
But I want you to know that  
I  
Will be waiting for  
YOU  
If you want me I'm close enough to find...  
In between the LINES  
I'll be waiting for  
YOU  
If you need me I'm close enough to find...  
In between the lines.  

I want you, that's pretty clear  
I'll love you whether we kiss or fight  
All my pain disappears  
When I see you walking by
