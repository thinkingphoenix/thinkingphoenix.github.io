---
title: The Boy, the Girl and the Big City
date: 2022-05-09 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [connection, friendship, city-life, warmth, strangers, hope]
---

**Phase I**

He stumbled once  
On the zebra crossing,  
His steps sounded anxious  
In the manner of his walking.  

Tall buildings were all around,  
People forgetting their names from stress,  
A city too beautiful for an outlander  
And yet sophisticated, having its own mess.  

Busy roads at 3 in the night,  
Busy people without a moment to spare.  
He paused on his tracks to heave out  
A lonely sigh of polluted air.  

"There, there..." came a voice  

**Phase II**

And a kind pat on the head.  
An amicable stranger stood  
With whom his road had met.  

"This world is my home"  
said she and the boy was intrigued.  
Like a good spirit she came  
A companion, a friend in need.  

The city grew less overwhelming  
With this uncanny girl, with a charming heart  
Stories were exchanged, bittersweet  
And the chapters from here did start.
