---
title: Breaking Point
date: 2021-01-07 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [bullying, pain, insecurity, heartbreak, tragedy, isolation]
---

They threw papers balls at her,  
Scribbled hatred on her desk.  
Was it because she was dusky  
Or didn't have a flaunting chest?  

They mocked every inch of her  
Her words soon morphed into tears  
That kept her company earnestly  
In a class full of youthful cheers.  

Iron bends and bends, when under strain  
But breaks when it's too much to endure.  
 Her beautiful brown wrist bled from a cut  
 When she decided she can't take it anymore.
