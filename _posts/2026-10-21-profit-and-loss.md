---
title: Profit/Loss
date: 2026-10-21 10:57:35 +0530
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [revelation, retrospection, introspection]
---

I admire science,   
And I forgot that mathematics    
Will still apply to emotions, beauty, all things artistic.    
For example, Profit and Loss.    
I still may fumble the formulae, I'm a lost cause.    
Because people do feel for profit,    
Love, precalculated, projected for benefits.    
I've bargained and then taken its loss,    
I've wept, sobbed and summarised all my flaws.    

This is why    
When love feels easy enough to breathe,     
That may not be a poetic sign.    
Fuuuucking idiot,     
It could be a parasite crawling its way inside,    
Fully disguised in the skin of a beautiful soul    
And could appear as a universe, while being a black hole.    

"nisemono"      
(Japanese) (adj./n./v.) :    
Meaning : counterfeit, conjured    
Up as an ersatz of things pure.    
For example, demonic    
That outwardly looks demure.    

I show a mirror and see them break,    
Not a thing else to be done,    
Their reflection, is what they'd hate.     

Hate, hate and hate     
I will just this once.    
I will rest in my hating den,    
For one last time and never again.     

Call my love limerence.    
At least even my limerence isn't pretense.    
I say "fucked up" in clear syllables each time    
And write it off in no schemes of half-rhymes.    

I don't believe in a destined human.    
But if you exist, I am so sorry.    
So sorry I obliterated myself    
Before you could find my body.    

I will remorph    
(Actuate my remorse)      
All beautiful things I've written for humans      
To match the ugliness of my heart that's burned.    

I will do this and then stop.    
I will mitigate all the loss.    

I will eradicate it all,    
Squeeze the wound and bleed it out,    
Exile all the evil within    
And assimilate all the good without.    

Henceforth, I will not hurt    
And I will not take hurt either.    
I won't let this build up inside of me.    
I shall be strong, superhumanly wiser.    

No tears to waste on things inconsequential,     
I'm gonna rip the heart out of my demons.    
I'm pouring their ashes into this urn.    
My 9th life has ended. This shall be my rebirth.
