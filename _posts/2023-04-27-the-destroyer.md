---
title: The Destroyer
date: 2023-04-27 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [anger, destruction, transition, betrayal, pain, dark]
---

The nurturer in me quits  
As the destroyer takes over.  

As my core turns to stone,  
Beating through the sorrow,  
You kill my emotions and ask  
Me to have hopes about tomorrow.  

My innocence is in ashes  
With remains of love that existed.  
It flows into the holy river  
And contaminates her providence.  

Same questions and same answers,  
No communication from what's within.  
I hold back my words, I stay quiet—  
I can't be a clown just to fit in.  

You think I'm wounded  
But I expect and endure worse  
And not bat an eyelid  
Because pain no longer hurts.  

And now I shall unveil  
The counterfeit you've become.  
I'll burn the web of deceit  
And let you crawl in the Sun.  

I'll take no more of this  
Toxic elixir that left me colder.  
The nurturer in me has quit  
As the destroyer has taken over.
