---
title: Matchbox
date: 2019-11-10 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [resilience, hope, healing, heart, pain, strength]
---

Your heart, a box of matches  
Has seen a lot, hasn't it?  
Some struck them  
To lighten your life  
Some struck them  
To see infernos rise  
(Fuck them)  
And hide the sticks moist by your tears  
Dead, obsolete through all these years  
Keep the ones left and hold your ground  
To light yourself when your time runs out.
