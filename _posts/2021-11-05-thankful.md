---
title: Thankful
date: 2021-11-05 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [gratitude, hope, peace, daily-life, mindfulness, contentment]

For mornings with sunlight  
And a day warm and bright  
For a breeze on hot noons  
And for nights when there's Moon,  
Let's spare a moment to be thankful.  

For a coffee cup to work harder  
When in thurst, a glass of water  
For a meal shared with mates  
Whenever back home and not late  
Let's spare a moment to be thankful.  

For every person who truly cares  
For peace and love in the atmosphere  
For a cozy bed to sleep in  
For dreams and hopes to believe in  
Let's spare a moment to be thankful.  

When tomorrow brings no worries  
And there's no need to hurry  
When there's true elation  
Without risks or limitation  
Let's spare a moment to be thankful.
