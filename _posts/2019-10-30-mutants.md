---
title: Mutants
date: 2019-10-30 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [dark, evolution, society, coping, emotions, numbness]
---

Sometimes, I have this awkward intuition  
Beneath our skin, we're turning into mutants.  
To survive this hostile world  
That first gets you hurt  
And then suggests remedies,  
To embrace your fucking enemies.  
And what do we do?  
We shake it off,  
As if it's nothing new.  
Pills to stop tears,  
Pills to feel numb.  
Pills to make us happy,  
Pills to fade our glum.  
And hence, our systems have changed,  
Even our emotions are two-faced.  
One outside, keeping us sane  
One within, lurking through our veins.   
We're a different species  
Colder, harsher and more efficient.  
At the tip of the evolution tree,  
Masked, enigmatic and more elegant.  
We exist appreciating this innate delusion  
Beneath our skin, we're turning into mutants.
