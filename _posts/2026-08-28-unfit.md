---
title: Unfit
date: 2026-08-28 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [self-doubt, love, despair, isolation]
---

I am  
Unfit for love.  

In moments of isolation  
Where I'm my unfortunate self,  
I unbutton the jacket of joy I wear  
And peel off my smile to reveal despair.  

"How long has this act been up?"  
"How long can this go on?"  
I ask the person in the mirror  
And walk away as he shivers.  

When did I die?   
I don't remember.  
Was I a child?  
Was I a teenager?  
Who/What turned me so lifeless?  
Maybe an amalgamation of all I saw,  
Into the me I am.  
Maybe the transumutation of my flaws  
Into the peace I have  
Now.  
Now, where I am hyper-aware  
Of myself and others.  
Now, where I rest in my lair  
And yearn for an endeavour.  

Endeavour, of understanding  
My grief and theirs.  
Endeavour, long-standing  
Where I'm the only heir  
Of my cursed fate.  
I open my gates  
For warmth, for the Sun, for starlight.  
But my cellular structure  
Has already evolved for frigid, cold nights.  

Love is a horrifying entity.  
It feeds on stimuli.  
It requires reassurance,  
It requires validation,  
It requires attention,  
It requires you to specialize  
In feeding stimuli  
Even when you are  
Holding onto dear life.  
This entity is my nightmare.  
For I cannot equal what it wants.  
It wants, wants and fucking wants,  
Even if you cease to exist providing.  
It wants you to die fighting.  

I have dueled life alone.  
I have not known  
What it means to be understood.  
Because my thoughts are within a quarantine.  
Very few can even access them,  
Much less decipher what they mean.  

If love means to constantly do, say and act,  
I would rather transcend it  
Such that it becomes a closely held pact  
That reinforces strength in vulnerability,  
That reinforces stability in uncertainty,  
That reinforces hope during sorrow,  
That fills all spaces hollow.  

They mistake entertainment for companionship.  
They want a mediator for their conflicts.  
They want to be loved when they can't love themselves.  
They put people in boxes and expect an affection endless.  

I am but a broken clown.  
My voice is faint, I cannot dance much.  
I am but a broken clown.  
Permanently maimed, like a blotched canvas.  
I still paint on myself even if others won't.  
I still sing tunes, to the most, unknown.  

I am not a liar.  
It is the truth that I digest so well  
And they cannot.  
That this beauty is all for naught  
If it is a farce, curated.  
I cannot force one  
To feel elated.  

My knees kiss the ground,  
Almost vengeful, I stare hevenwards  
At the sky above.  
I am  
Unfit for love.
