---
title: Awake
date: 2017-07-20 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [nightmares, insomnia, pain, fear, sleep, darkness]
---

Sleep or slumber  
Can't heal or ease  
Me, but only make me feel  
Nothing, as long as I see  
Nothing, as long as I hear  
Nothing, I no longer fear  
Nothing, exactly what I need  
Nothing, exactly what I mean.  
Nightmares now keep me sane  
In shut-eyes that numb my pain  
How I hate  
When there comes a daybreak  
Of all I'm afraid  
Is to be awake.
