---
title: Copycat
date: 2023-05-11 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [identity, conformity, mask, isolation, self-reflection]
---

To belong where you are,  
To hide all your scars,  
You observe everyone well-  
Everyone except yourself.  

Crushed under their impact.  
You're just another copycat.  

Blank, forced smiles,  
Rehearsed gleam in eyes.  
Pain, suppressed within,  
Shrewd lies to fit in.  

In every calculated act,  
You're just another copycat.  

You've faked happiness,  
The core of this mess.  
Now you don't know  
What to do with sorrow.  

So do what you're good at.  
You're just another copycat.
