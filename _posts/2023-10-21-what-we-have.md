---
title: What We Have
date: 2023-10-21 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [heaven, romance, love, intimacy, soulmate]
---
And here she is, on my chest,  
Unaware of how my heart rushes  
Each second her breath sweeps my skin.  
And her mehendi patterned hand touches  
My cheek, and through it, my soul,  
Which she owns now. She appeared  
Into my life as if the Gods took pity on  
My yearning for love, abandoned over the years.  
She's warm. Like a bonfire at a frosty mountain,  
She thaws the ice this world left on me  
With her efforts, her innocent affection,  
She makes my Earth feel heavenly.  
This, is the woman I've wanted,  
This, is who I belong to.  
This, is the page for my verses,  
This, is whom I'll sing songs to.
