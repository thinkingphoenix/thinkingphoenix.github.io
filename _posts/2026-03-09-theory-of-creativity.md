---
title: Theory of Creativity
date: 2026-03-09 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [creativity, theory, suffering, art]
---

I'm an artist.  
I'm valued as long as I create.  

I've seen others receive   
Flowers just for existing,  
While I find myself   
Slowly sinking.  
Sinking into my tunnel    
Of fears that take forms.  
Forms with hands.  
Hands with sharp claws.  
Claws breaking into my skin.  
Skin that shows no signs,  
Of how they tear me day out, day in.  
I battle my self-doubt,  
To be deemed eligible,   
I write how I feel  
To not be invisible.  
Like an addict I weave words  
For the next jolt of admiration.  
To hit that high others get  
From shallow validation.  
I'm strong but not everyday;   
I sit here, defenseless,  
In wait of the vile creatures  
That crawl into this fortress  
That I've built for myself.  
My armour is worn out.    
Should I cry for help?  
I can hear their hunger.  
I will mould my misery—  
Into what you call poetry—  
As they drag me down under,  

As my lungs clog and  
I asphyxiate.  
As I said, I'm an artist.  
I'm valued    
As long as I create.
