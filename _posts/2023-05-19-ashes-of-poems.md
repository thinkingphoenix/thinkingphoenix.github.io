---
title: Ashes of Poems
date: 2023-05-19 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [grief, loss, dark, numbness, tragedy]
---

Ages back, I could make bouquets  
Of flower-like words, that I  
Would have loved to give you now.  
But my nursery didn't survive.  

My eyes see beauty no more.  
Murky images of emotions  
Make their way into my  
Unlit floor of the ocean.  

I see in shades of black.  
That's the being I've become.  
The poet's skin that I live in,  
And this heart, cold, numb.  

The living portions of me  
Sense a charm in you.  
All was burnt by this monster.  
I only have ashes of poems to offer.
