---
title: The Tormentor
date: 2023-06-08 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [dark, trauma, resilience, inner-turmoil, introspection]
---

I'm warm-blooded and cold-hearted.  
My silence disrupts "cheerful" people  
Who hide venom behind their smile  
And invisible claws, sharp and lethal.  

I've learnt only to obey.  
I've learnt only to fear.  
Only to hold back screams  
When my skin tears.  

They all just harm.  
Themselves a bit, and others more.  
Their outside charm  
Makes me sick, I know their core.  

Punched and kicked,  
Thrashed with sticks,  
Doubled up, I can taste  
My own blood: I'm being  
Punished for mistakes.  

Young then, I cried like others,  
Cursing everything that exists.  
No one would ever know  
Why there were bruises on my lips.  

Couldn't kill myself;  
I was either scared to make cuts  
Or greedy to survive  
Or was an optimistic fuck.  

I learnt to believe.  
I learnt to love.  
Only to see this tormentor  
Hidden within their husk.
