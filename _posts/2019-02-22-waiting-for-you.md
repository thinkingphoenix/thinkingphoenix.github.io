---
title: Waiting For You
date: 2019-02-22 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [longing, absence, home, memories, sorrow]
---

The looking glass  
On my dressing table  
Misses the charm of your  
Whirlpool navel  
Come back home  
We're waiting for you.  

The coffee mugs  
On my kitchen maze  
Miss the smear of your  
Lipstick stain  
Come back home  
We're waiting for you.  

The six strings  
On my guitar's bridge  
Miss your strum when you  
Played those riffs  
Come back home  
We're waiting for you.  

The morning birds  
Near my window pane  
Miss your voice sweeter  
Than sugarcane  
Come back soon  
We're waiting for you.
