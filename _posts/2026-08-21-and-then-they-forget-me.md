---
title: And then they forget me
date: 2026-08-21 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [sorrow, love, lament, defiance]
---

I  
Always  
Know how they  
Disintegrate.  
These wretched stories  
Of ardent love, longing.  
In my cavern, pale fire burns,  
Leaving ashes in its wake of  
My wrath. Respite. Remorse. Remains of  
Humans allowed proximity  
To me. Vague divinity  
Enshrouding their instincts,  
Unresolved conflicts.  
I shelter each.  
And then they  
Forget  
Me,  
A sore  
Loser of   
Love. God, I can't  
Stand this endless loop.  
Destroy my heart before  
I give away more as food.  
Seen enough, done enough, I quit.  
I can pawn no more in your gambit.  
You cursed this existence on me,  
A thinking anomaly.  
This torn reality,  
Where I live, exist,  
Blood on my teeth.  
And I still 
Dare to  
Breathe.
