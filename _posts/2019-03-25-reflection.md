---
title: Reflection
date: 2019-03-25 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [reflection, self-doubt, deception, pain, existential, hindi]
---

He imitates me  
From my cladding to core.  
He intimidates me,  
He's making me unsure  
Of my own existence.  
I'm about to give up,  
To plead for remittance,  
Just when a broken leaf  
Fell and he rippled away  
Clearing the deception.  
Sinister at my feet  
A foot deep, like endless rays,  
My shallow reflection.
