---
title: Non-Consumable
date: 2026-07-23 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [despair, lovelorn, bitterness]
---
With each day that adds to my age,  
I move closer to despair.  
"Is someone meant for me?"  
I see the question as unfair.  
Neither do I ask others,  
Nor do I sit and ponder.  
When I do, I bleed words,  
And they see them on screens or paper,  
The thoughts I discard to be faced later.  
About how it's all a marketing gimmick to me.  
Because how can I buy this concept  
That I can be, beyond my flesh, seen truly?  
In the digital realm that inundates with stimuli,  
In the physical realm that titillates the eyes,  
What am I if not another commodity?  
A snack for their thoughts, emotional needs?  
It's a Darwin's world.  
And competing for love isn't my forte.  
I'm enslaved/emancipated by art. I am   
Another dead poet, running from his own fate.
