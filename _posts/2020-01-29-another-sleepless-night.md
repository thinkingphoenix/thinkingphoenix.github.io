---
title: Another Sleepless Night
date: 2020-01-29 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [romance, longing, affection, warmth, vulnerability, late-night]
---

It's quite late night...  
She dozed off, borrowing my sleep.  
Leaving behind intrigue  
And a warmth over my heart to keep.  
Here, again, vulnerable,  
I write away what I can't contain.  
I told her after all,  
I let myself go, unchained.  
Jealous, yes I feel  
Of her cigarettes that meet her lips.  
Hope she smiles from there,  
Lets me keep it as a gift.  
And when she wakes up,  
The weaver of my dreams...  
She'll find out, to me,  
Exactly what she means.
