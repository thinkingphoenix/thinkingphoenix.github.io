---
title: Once Again
date: 2018-01-02 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [romance, longing, affection, nostalgia, hope, feeling]
---

She sits on the bench before me  
Once again.  
My heart, it plays the old symphony  
Once again.  
Her chirrups stir up a new melody  
Once again.  
On cliched jokes, I laugh wildly  
Once again.  
The window-breeze strokes her opened pony  
Once again.  
My eyes to hers, tell a story  
Once again.
