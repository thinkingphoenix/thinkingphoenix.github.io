---
title: Thunderstruck
date: 2022-04-19 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [resilience, strength, transformation, power, rebirth, determination]
---

Darkness enveloped the skies, the grounds,  
The air stood still, surrendering to anticipation.  
Flashes appear, tearing through the night,  
And descend towards my instigation.  

"Bring me life, I'm in need  
Give me wounds, make me bleed  
Bring me joy, show me pain  
Teach me how to live again",  
I bawled at the Lightning,  
The heavenly wrath that purifies,  
To reconstruct my soul tonight  
To nullify my truths and lies.  

To stand like this light, against adversities, adamant,  
Tazing the pain, fear and doubts.  
To be sole yet supreme, solivagant  
Live a life to be proud about.  

I'm Thunderstruck  
In me courses the will  
To hold my sword high  
Against the demons to kill.
