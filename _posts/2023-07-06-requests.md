---
title: Requests
date: 2023-07-06 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [longing, desire, love, romance]
---

Step into me, wander in me,  
For I long to be discovered  
By your eyes, for who I am,
Understood by words unuttered.  

Find a reason to stay here,  
For I long for your presence.  
Like your pencils on my workspace  
Or my night clothes carrying your scent.  

We could find joy in trivial matters—  
Let's cook together, I'll assist and learn.  
Let's play video games, the enemies shall burn.  
Let's just dance on the terrace in the rain.  
Or brew coffee on workdays to remain sane.  

Thoughts are scattered like berries  
In a farm, when I think of you.  
I pick each to put in this basket  
To make a fine drink for you.  
Drink it to know how I feel  
Everytime your name appears  
Anywhere, including my mind,  
How you have me endeared.
