---
title: Ashes and Tears
date: 2023-03-01 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [grief, heartbreak, isolation, pain, disillusionment, struggle]
---

That 4 am kickback  
Of the crippled core;  
A tray brimmed with ash,  
While eyes with tears amid the smoke.  

There've been voices lately,  
Possibly the eroding soul  
Screaming questions vaguely  
Unanswered, in the mind they echo.  

Ever seen your passion reflect?  
You won't, and it's an axiom.  
What are you rummaging for, so bereft  
In your empty bag of affection?  

As people walk in and out,  
They do so with a chunk of you.  
Fucking imbecile, they're not devout—  
Just abating the hurtful truth.  

The truth, that you still lack  
But your shoulders always bore.  
You lie on the fallen ash  
In eyes, the tears and in air, the smoke.
