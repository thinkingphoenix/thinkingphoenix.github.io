---
title: I Write Because
date: 2026-10-20 00:00:00 +0530
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [apathy, pain, expression]
---
I write because  
I cannot scream.  
This is where my grief finds voice,  
While all that destroys me may rejoice.  

I write because  
I want to weep.  
This is how I shed tears of blood,  
While all that hurts me forms a flood.  

I write because  
No one knows.  
This is how I let my hurt known,  
While I from within, turn to stone.  

I write because  
I am helpless.  
For I am misunderstood by all,  
While I count the steps to my fall.  

I write because  
It fucking hurts.  
This is the way I release pain,  
While as a prey for my demons I've lain.  

I write because  
I've been sold for parts.  
I laugh as I am picked for pieces,  
While I write away my perfect pretense.  

I write because  
You slept through this.  
This fate that fucks me over  
While I bawl, shriek and holler.  

I write because  
Your apathy triumphs.  
My eyes change their expression,  
As in you changes my reflection.  

I write because  
You act like an ectotherm.  
And I'm the tail that's cut off  
In your scheme to live on.
