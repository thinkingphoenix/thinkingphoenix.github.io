---
title: Religion of Words
date: 2019-09-15 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [literature, art, unity, words, poetry, feeling]
---

The religion of words,  
Heeds no last name...  
Alike it belongs  
To the king, to the slave.   

True literature has only one creed  
Inherited by the ones, the ones who feel  
And limn on paper, portraits in written ink  
Sometimes making us laugh, sometimes think,  
Or sometimes turn off lights and weep,  
Harking back stories from memory's keep.  

The religion of words  
And the others aren't same  
Alike it belongs  
To the king, to the slave.
