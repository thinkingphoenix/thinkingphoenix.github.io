---
title: Blade
date: 2018-02-20 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [darkness, betrayal, heartbreak, loss, strangers, pain]
---

The edge of the blade  
On my sweating throat  
Rests; you can save me  
Just how you once chose  
But now we're strangers;  
I fear your glance, cold,  
As you heave a sigh...  
And the steel, a deep stroke.
