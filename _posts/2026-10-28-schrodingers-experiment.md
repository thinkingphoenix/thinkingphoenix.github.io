---
title: Schrodinger's Experiment
date: 2026-10-28 04:20:32 +0530
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [distress, paradox, contradiction]
---

This love that exists simultaneously   
And does not,   
Leaves my hands cold, draws blood intravenously,   
With a heart to rot.   

You speak love,   
I hear convenience.  
You preach care,  
I learn indifference.  
You plan futures,  
I see evanescence.  

How can humans be such a paradox?  
I keep enquiring myself over and over  
Trying to find an answer,  
Something to defend your cruelty with.  
Something to ascend from this insanity with.  

Willfully or not, I bear this torment;   
Willfully or not, you hear me lament.  
This is a bizarre Schrodinger's experiment.  
This love that fluctuates in its being,    
Between acts of togetherness and leaving.  

You push away,  
You pull me in.  

I stand firm  
But there is no ground.  
I ask you to search your feelings,  
You return 404 : none found.  
And I scream in my head   
That you are   
Lying,  
Denying,  
Defying.  
Just because you know  
I will plead at your door.  
Just because you know  
I will get on all fours  
Like the animal I am.  
An animal that tries to understand,  
To comprehend how love works :  
It is beyond my fucking logic.  
My story accursed to be tragic.  

What else do I make of it?   
You believe in actions   
And your own actions  
Contradict your intention.  

Willfully or not, I too will now pretend;   
Willfully or not, since you prefer things that end.  
This is a bizarre Schrodinger's experiment.  
This love that dies as soon as my grip loosens,  
While I keep making friends with the bruises  
On my mind    
And scream in my head   
That you are   
Lying,  
Denying,  
Defying.  
Just because you know  
That I will not hate.  
Just because you know  
That I disintegrate  
And return to you immaculate.  
I urged you on and on  
To communicate.  
So that I could hold onto  
Your keepsake  
And wait for you to  
Open your gates.  
But you shut me out,     
Unfazed  
At my pain.    
So I weep no more.  
No. I will weep no more.  
I will bleed no more.  
I will lose sleep no more.  
I will refuse to eat no more.  

I will breathe.  
I will not let anything affect me.  
Not you. Not life.   
Not truth. Not lies.  

All I see is humor.  
All I see is hypocrisy.  
People claiming to love,  
And doing so in secrecy.  

Because a yearner is only subject to hurt.  
How dare I expect to be loved like a human  
When all I know is to be discarded, disowned and disturbed.  

Fuck this all, fuck the Schrodinger's cat.  
It may have lived or died;   
But I know where to be buried at.  
And I will bring myself to life again.  
I will need nothing, no one.   
I will be alive again.
