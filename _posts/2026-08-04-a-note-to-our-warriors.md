---
title: A Note to Our Warriors
date: 2026-08-04 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [democracy, rebellion, protest, uprising]
---


I write in English because  
We are still prisoners of colonialism.  
I was taught that English  
Is key to professionalism.  
But today I use this  
So that it reaches all eyes,  
The young and brave minds.  
I gave up on politics,  
Never on democracy.  
The flawed but the only right choice.  
Now I see it in shambles,  
And hence I unmute my voice.  

Here is the recipe for flawless subjugation :  
1. Emotional manipulation of the masses  
2. Industrialization of education  
3. Lack of livelihood opportunities  
4. Journalism and reality having disparities  
5. Surveillance on free speech  
6. Crippling the youth with liabilities    
7. Corruption on microscopic levels  
8. Politicians treated as royalty  
9. Violence for questioning power  
10. Survival goal becomes to flee  
11. Hobson's choice when voting  
12. Leaders who grow on lies and money.    

This recipe is already being cooked :    
The firewood being our hopes and dreams.  
The fire being the oaths they took    
And the smoke, our muffled screams.  

I am but an ordinary man.  
I can be jailed, maimed or killed. 
But if I can inspire us to keep fighting,  
To stand against dictatorship, I will.  
Because each citizen present at a protest,  
Is our valuable warrior.  
They have just as much to lose.   
Parents at home praying  
Not to see their child as news.  
They have just as much at risk.  
Careers, ambitions, words they jotted    
Onto a future bucket list.  

It is time to wake the fuck up.  
The flood has reached our doorsteps.  
If it concerns some, it concerns all,  
Together, we must demolish their walls.
