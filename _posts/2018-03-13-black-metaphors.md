---
title: Black Metaphors
date: 2018-03-13 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [darkness, sorrow, despair, metaphors, grief, angst]
---

Broken voice of a childhood lulllaby  
Plangent in the angst of weeping breeze  
Severes through every heart, like a scythe;  
The open wounds are briny now.  

Hope dies; it can't cope anymore  
Even I am out of black metaphors.
