---
title: Cannibalism
date: 2019-11-19 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [dark, pain, emotions, internal-conflict, destruction, heartbreak]
---

I've been a witness  
To cannibalism  
Where a feeling consumes the other  
As I stand helpless  
To the macabre rhythm  
Of my meeker emotions seeking cover,  
But in vain.  
They're slain  
By predators of disdain.  
I stand in chains  
Wondering how much methadone  
Will obtund this pain.  
My heart's in pieces.  
Each with its own mannerism.  
I've indeed been a witness  
To something akin to cannibalism.
