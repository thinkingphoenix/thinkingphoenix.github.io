---
title: Feelings Broken Free
date: 2020-02-08 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [love, romance, devotion, intimacy, enchantment, warmth]
---

Black, her favourite color  
Black, what I see when I blink.  
She laughs, holding my heart in her palm,  
She's an enchantress, a lovely minx.  

Her eyes, from underneath the glasses  
Cast the spell of Aphrodite  
In my vain she stirs ecstasy,  
Goosebumps like stalagmites.  

Deeper and deeper I venture  
Into her persona, I can't get enough  
Of hearing her voice, words and love  
Before her, my pride is debuffed.  

Her soul feels like home to dwell in;  
I dawdle off my thoughts while we talk...  
From plane earth to a vale of flowers  
Where I feel her... like the sun after snowfall.  

My chest feels warmer when she utters  
My nickname in her honey-like voice.  
My lips forget pain and smile  
I'm drenched in love, I have no choice.  

How my heart desires, to enmesh you in my arms  
How my lips crave, to melt into yours,  
For I'm lost, but found a sweet home...  
And my guitar strings, a beautiful chord.
