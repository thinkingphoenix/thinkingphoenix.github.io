---
title: Schrodinger's Man
date: 2023-04-22 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [existential, isolation, darkness, internal-conflict, ambiguity, struggle]
---

The dawn never made it  
Into the void he lived in,  
Bereft of light, isolated.  
Darkness in which feet sink in,  
Inundates and rises,  
A centimeter each day.  
Suffocating silence muffles  
The shattering noises made  
By the shackled-up heart  
That dares to live, to be freed  
From hate, lust and greed  
Prevalent outside the void.  
Is he dead?  
Or alive the way he wanted to be?  
Is the heart still shackled?  
Or living, nurtured and freed.
