---
title: The Cave
date: 2023-02-04 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [isolation, darkness, despair, pain, regret]
---

The cave that I reside in—  
Strands of light encroach  
Amid darkness, where demons hide in  
Nurtured by despair, disdain,  
Delusions of deceased dreams,  
Dim-lit but discernible pain  
And decrepit notions of life—  
How it should have, could have  
Been. It burns my flesh, the light  
Like silence broken by din  
In the cave I reside in.
