---
title: Death
date: 2019-05-26 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [death]
---

Death has her own sweetness.
Wiping it all clean,
She leaves a serene
And silent neatness.

From our servitude of joys and sorrows,
From our insides, hollowed,
She wins us salvation;
A loving arm
In the dark of devastation.

When the world puts out our eyes,
She rocks us to endless sleep.
In her lap, dreams lie,
Both blissful and unseen.

Some are terrorised.
Some are mesmerised.
Some are left speechless.

She asks no alibis,
She hates to catechise,
Death has her own sweetness.
