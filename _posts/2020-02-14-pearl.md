---
title: Pearl
date: 2020-02-14 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [love, devotion, treasure, destiny, healing, soul]
---

From the ocean's bed, an unventured depth,  
I found a pearl, pristine in itself.  

I tell you, it's not an ornament to wear  
For it's charm is beyond mere eyes of a man  
It's a jewel to be treasured, looked after,  
Away from shallow hands that don't understand  

It's worth that cannot be quoted, but felt.  
I found a pearl, pristine in itself.  

I stood by the beach, making castles of sand,  
It seems the oceans washed it ashore for me,  
To be discovered, be awestruck by, be dazed.  
I felt no less than a child of destiny  

As in my palms, like dewdrops it melts  
I found a pearl, pristine in itself.  

Consuming my darkness, it illuminates my heart,  
As it's charm seeps into my soul, I feel bliss.  
Making me want to love everything, even myself,  
As if my tears were wiped, planted with a kiss.  

It's a sacred  trinket, meant to be closely held  
I found a pearl, pristine in itself.
