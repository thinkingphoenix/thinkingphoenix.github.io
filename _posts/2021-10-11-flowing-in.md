---
title: Flowing In
date: 2021-10-11 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [agony, despair, expectations, struggle, isolation, mental-health]
---

I sink deeper  
Under the weight  
Of expectations  

I feel weaker  
I suffocate  
I'm losing patience  

It's hard sometimes  
To be like everyone  
It's hard to smile  
When I feel dysfunctional  

Just when I think there's escape  
All this agony keeps  
Flowing in  
Flowing in  

How strong should I build the gates  
For this agony to stop  
Flowing in  
Flowing in  

I fall silent  
Everyone's joy  
Won't let me scream loud  

When all vibrance  
That hides my voids  
Won't let me bleed out  

Sometimes it's not  
Worth struggling to be free  
Trapped in the thoughts  
To be beaten or to beat  

Just when I think there's escape  
All this agony keeps  
Flowing in  
Flowing in  

How long should my demons wait  
For this agony to stop  
Flowing in  
Flowing in  

Sorrow is on the doorstep  
I've no tears to offer  
I wait for dawn to come  
Then think why bother
