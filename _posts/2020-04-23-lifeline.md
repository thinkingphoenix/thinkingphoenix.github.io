---
title: Lifeline
date: 2020-04-23 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [disillusionment, heartbreak, cynicism, loneliness, coping, darkness]
---

Stuck in the age   
Of empty promises  
Seeping through this  
Inner hollowness.  
Love is a guest,  
Like a lifeline  
For a fixed time.  

Been at fault, always  
Believing everyone's lies  
In my sky of oblivion;  
Grey clouds they hide.  
So malign, so divine,  
Like a lifeline  
For a fixed time.  

All this makes me much  
Better at taking jokes  
The way they're meant  
To be laughed off till you choke,  
Holding a false hope,  
Like a lifeline  
For a fixed time.  

I see and remember  
What forms today's reality,  
A layer of fake over all,  
A sheen over moral brutality.  
Or rather practicality?  
Like a lifeline  
For a fixed time.
