---
title: Stories
date: 2017-08-03 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [memories, scars, solitude, reflection, pain, past]
---

A few of them make me smile  
Most of them choke me  
Some urge my pen to write  
In verses, how time broke me  

And gave me invisible scars  
These are stories buried in my heart.  

In evenings when nobody's home  
Or in a presence where no one speaks  
I witness this fucking syndrome  
That points at how my peace is breached  

Through the door I erred to leave ajar  
These are stories buried in my heart.
