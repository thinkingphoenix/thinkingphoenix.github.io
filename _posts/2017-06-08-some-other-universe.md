---
title: Some Other Universe
date: 2017-06-08 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [solitude, insanity, pain, realism, darkness]
---

And almost in some other universe,  
In spaces left in the dark by the Sun,  
Lives my soul, draped in chaotic solitude,  
To hold myself from falling I'm the only one.  
From falling into the depths of depravity,  
Pitch black arms of insanity.  
Of what people speak I don't fathom  
When they describe warm emotions,  
Of love and its notions.   
Because this is all I've come across—  
Selfish interests, lust, and superficial lives.  
Beneath bewitching countenances, cursed intentions hide.  
I've come to accept the world yet it's ironic  
That it breeds a warm heart only to store it in frost and mist.
