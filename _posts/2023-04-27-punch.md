---
title: Punch
date: 2023-04-27 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [rage, anger, struggle, pain, isolation, defiance]
---

It surges through me  
From my seething soul  
To the tips of the knuckles  
The rage swallows me whole.  

Infuriated, at these  
Intangible enemies,  
I punch my way through life.  

People, pulverized  
Under greed, guilt, gluttony.  
I'm stuck here forever  
Holding onto my agony.  

My knuckles bleed,  
These wounds never heal  
As I punch my way through life.  

Violence is unfair  
And so is this world.  
How they put in separate boxes  
The happy and the hurt.  

My struggles unknown,  
And my fears known,  
I punch my way through life.
