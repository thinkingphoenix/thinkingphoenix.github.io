---
title: Lip Language
date: 2023-11-18 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [love, romance, intensity, kiss]
---
With her touch she has turned me  
From a stone statue to a human.  
Tear drops trickled down my cheek,  
As she sculpted my world into a new one.  

Gentle winds swept through us,  
Her ambrosial aroma absorbed in it,  
That both soothes and sets me alight  
As we hold each other and unite our lips.  

A kiss fluctuating between  
Insanity and innocence.  
She bites my lips in ecstacy,  
Then nurses them with patience.  

We smooch madly as if starved.  
Indeed we are for this intimacy  
And affection. I see in her eyes  
How she holds my reflection delicately.  

At loss of words, I'm smiling.  
Spellbound by each other,  
We communicate by touch,  
Me, and my lover.
