---
title: Let's Dance
date: 2020-03-23 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [dance, fun, passion, excitement, freedom, connection]
---

Pass me your hand,  
Let me hold it like all I've got,  
Set your feet loose,  
Whether you like the beats or not.  
Let's dance.  

Four shots down,  
We're all set to hit the floor.  
Let your waist go haywire,  
Make steps like you've never before.  
Let's dance.  

We're both insane,  
As the others will discover,  
As we amateurs steal the show,  
Let them wonder if we're friends or lovers.  
Let's dance.  

Shy, you're not.  
Nor am I, a lethal combination.  
Breaking whatever joints we have,  
We'll simplify every complication.  
Let's dance..!
