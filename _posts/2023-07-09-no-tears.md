---
title: No Tears
date: 2023-07-09 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [tears, numbness, despair]
---

No tears left for me;  
I can't let out my sorrow.  
Everything that I've seen  
Has turned my insides hollow.  

People appear/disappear,  
Driving nails into my coffin.  
The darkness suffocates,  
While silences keep mocking.  

And I've now become  
Accustomed to my grave.  
Barely alive, mostly numb,  
Given in to my fate.  

I'm no more able to  
Scream or weep,  
Stay up or sleep.  
It should worry me but   
It facilitates peaceful life—  
Nobody has to know I'm fucked up—    
As I'm fully functional outside.  

Have I achieved finally  
What I always wanted to?  
This state of indifference  
As I keep being torn in two.  

Humour is all I feel,  
As I laugh off my sorrow.  
No tears will leave me,  
For my insides are now hollow.
