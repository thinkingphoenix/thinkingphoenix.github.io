---
title: Dirt Cheap Love
date: 2026-10-20 02:29:26 +0530
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [satire, pain]
---

It's a sale, grab this deal today!  
We have love for dirt cheap,   
Made of exquisite ingredients,  
Fully compliant, fully obedient.  
What we have here is a heart,  
At a glance it's good as new!  
Okay, it's been dropped a times few,  
Or maybe trampled over, then stitched and glued.  
But hey, it shows *almost* no residue  
Of hurt. This pitch black being its natural color.  
This is a poet's heart, now isn't it fancy?  
It can fashion charming words together,  
Just feed it a morsel of care, and it stops being bitter.  
Or don't, it doesn't matter, as it's FULLY functional!    
It's a bit cold to the touch,  
But I guarantee you it's living!  
If it starts bleeding, burn the injury, it'll be fine.    
If it stops beating, extract its misery, it'll be fine.  
Don't feed it hate, at least not more than it can hold,  
As it might malfunction and turn it tenfold.  
With basic caution, it's a fine surrogate  
If you wish to be entertained.  
We also accept returns!  
If it doesn't work, just bring it back,  
No, not dead, decayed or stabbed.   
Haha, for that we're not responsible.  
So what are you waiting for?  
Steal the deal while it's fresh and hot!
