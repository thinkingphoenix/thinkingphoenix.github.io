---
title: The Earrings
date: 2023-03-22 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [heartbreak, nostalgia, longing, regret, abandoned, grief]
---

A pair of modest earrings  
With tiny feathers, lay at rest  
In the Goan market an evening.  
As travellers sifted through  
And haggled prices, someone  
Was picturing these on the ears  
Of a dainty girl with hair undone  
Who lived one city away,  
Yet dwelled in his musings,  
Loved and hankered for.  
The earrings he bought truly  
Were his first time of this desire.  

It's been months since.  
If the earrings could talk,  
They'd lament about the broken link,  
The failed attempts to be gifted.  

It's been months since.  
If the earrings could weep,  
They'd do so amid abandoned things  
Of his backyard,  
Seething under the sun,  
Rotting in the rain.  

A pair of modest earrings  
With withered feathers, lay at rest,  
Now given up on people's feelings  
Swept into nothingness by cold breezes.
