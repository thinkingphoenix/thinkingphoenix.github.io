---
title: Care
date: 2018-03-27 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [darkness, pain, isolation, despair, apathy, reflection]
---

Neither the dark that feeds on my fears  
Nor the walls that drown my pleas  
Neither the ornaments of ordeals I wear  
Nor the salt seas on my numb knees  
Neither the shadows that seep through my skin  
Nor the wounds that I love to see bleed.  
I should've, though I never dared  
Never asked myself, why no one shared  
This pain? I'm not a seeker...  
When I don't care... 'bout myself either.
