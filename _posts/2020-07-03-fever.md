---
title: Fever
date: 2020-07-03 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [pain, isolation, longing, fever, struggle, vulnerability]
---

Rain hits the mud, all night outside  
I sit still on my evening chair, ears towards  
The elegy of droplets thumping by  
My balcony's sunshade, on my heart like steel swords.  

Shadows caress my ghashes,  
Saltwater on cheeks turns dry  
My body burns and turns to ashes  
The fever rises oh rises high.  

Nights are when I scream  
So silent, so unheard by  
People who always seem  
To be there, to be holding light.  

Crescent in the sky scatters  
Luminescence over awake eyes  
My body burns and turns to ashes  
The fever rises oh rises high.  

I fear to let my loved ones  
Sense this carnage within me  
I squirm until the daylight comes  
And mollifies the thoughts killing me.  

Till then...  
Someone hears me groan  
Someone reaches up to me  
Someone who's arms feel home  
Someone who doesn't seem to leave.  

That someone really matters...  
But assumes my words only lie  
My body burns and turns to ashes  
The fever rises oh rises high.
