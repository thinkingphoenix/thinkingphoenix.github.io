---
title: Renege
date: 2017-07-27 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [broken-promises, solitude, betrayal, pain, reflection, despair]
---

Every dream weathered away  
I haunt in that forsaken place  
Where people mill around; night and day  
All looking happy; in their own pace  
Some ask me what I have to say  
I smile instead,  
And shake my head.  
And when they're gone,  
I flip that page  
That reminds me of  
How people renege.
