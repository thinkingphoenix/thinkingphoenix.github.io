---
title: Invincibility
date: 2021-09-14 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [vulnerability, pain, darkness, struggle, honesty, grief]
---

Evenings still pass by  
Staining my purlieu in rosy hue  
I knew how it always was like  
To see the Sun give up on you.  

Dusk falls, the demons creep in  
To scavenge on what remains of  
My heart. I've stopped pleading;  
Been too tired to stay strong.  

Black flowers bloom  
And blind my eyes to sleep  
I disintegrate into doom  
At the edge of my empty dream.  

My mask wears off at times  
Making my turmoil visible  
A few tears some nights,  
After all, I'm not invincible.
