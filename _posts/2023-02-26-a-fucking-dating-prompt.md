---
title: A Fucking Dating Prompt
date: 2023-02-26 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [cynicism, modern-love, identity, honesty, disillusionment]
---

Lost in this marketplace  
Of thousands waiting to be auctioned,  
Am I just another face?  
Amid the flamboyant distortions.  

I seek not your approval or validation—  
Perceive beyond your discernment.  
On the walls of time I'm but a caricature,  
But I might be the one you intended.
