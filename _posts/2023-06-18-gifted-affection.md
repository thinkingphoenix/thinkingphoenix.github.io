---
title: Gifted Affection
date: 2023-06-18 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [personal, affection, companionship]
---
A spring that melts the ice  
From the subzero of my heart.  
Warm sunshine filling in  
Golden strokes in my art.  

I stand, baffled—  
I'm not familiar with affection.  
Like a playful kitten  
She walks towards my direction.  

We're seemingly two of a kind,  
Our similarities are outlandish.  
But she's injured, she has fallen.  
Like I did before. We're acrophobic.  

I will let her know that  
There exist people happy to   
Embrace her for who she is.  
Among them, one I can be too.  

I'm late in her life,  
She's late in mine.  
I will make up for it,  
Slowly, steadily with time.
