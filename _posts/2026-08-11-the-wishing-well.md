---
title: The Wishing Well
date: 2026-08-11 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [wishing-well, longing, devotion, protection, love, hindi]
---

I wish I could be  
The pages of your old notebooks.  
I could feel the caress of your palm,  
Your gaze  
And feel how you felt from  
Your letters’ shapes.  

I wish I could be  
The piercing on your cheek.  
I would know when your tears fall  
And bathe   
In them, be sanctified and for  
Your next smile, restlessly wait.  

I wish I could be  
CDs of your favourite artists.   
You would reach for me, need me,  
Listen to me.  
I would smile invisibly, soothing  
You with melodies.  

I wish I could be  
The band t-shirt you wear.  
I would draw friendly glances on you,  
Hear your heart beat,  
Carry your scent on me  
And with you, cozily fall asleep.  

I wish I could be  
The cigarette on your lips.  
I would become a habit for you.  
Set ablaze,  
I would be sorry to harm you  
But happy to numb your pain.  

I wish I could be   
Your looking glass. I would  
Admire you at times you forgot to.  
Your reflection  
I would etch upon myself  
For eternity five more seconds.  

I wish I could be  
The pendant on your neck.  
I would be your lucky charm,  
Guiding little  
Fortunes your way and be stained  
When you eat popsicles.  

I wish I could be  
A safehouse in your dreamscape.  
Dreams can often become nightmares.  
Fortify you,  
Off the claws of carnivorous creatures,  
I would hide you.  

Ah, I wish.  
To have known you long ago,  
I would have stopped,  
Your descent into insanity.  
You could have taught  
Me to let love win over vanity.   

But all wishes are not granted,  
Not necessarily.  
But I will toss these coins into the  
Wishing well.  
For you, I will always be  
Wishing well.
