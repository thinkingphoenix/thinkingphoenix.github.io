---
title: Hermit
date: 2017-12-26 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [hermit, solitude, pain, emotional-guard, grief, mask]
---

I remember yesterday's afternoon  
When one of her friends asked me,  
"Become a hermit, have you?"  
Unscathed, I parried their joke smartly  
Putting on a poker-face, masking residues  
Of pain in eyes that shine darkly.  

Yes, I've learnt finally  
To put a lock on my heart  
So that no one may enter again  
To put a filter on my eyes  
And keep her dreams far away.  

Like a hermit  
I preach to my own self  
With a vestige of vermillion  
Dabbed on my forehead.
