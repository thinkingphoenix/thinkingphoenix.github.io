---
title: Illusions
date: 2020-12-10 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [illusions, heartbreak, longing, love, dreams, pain, devotion]
---

I open my eyes  
And you're in front of me.  
I'm lit with joy  
As your eyes follow me.  
I reach up to you  
But my heart seethes  
This might be a dream,  
But it still believes  
That your aroma is all true,  
That in this moment, it's really you.  
That those eyes admit  
That they do want me,  
That the wait is over,  
That the river meets the sea.  
But I'm still afraid  
To close my eyes.  
Fear you may disappear  
When I open my eyes.  
Then you step towards me,  
I fill you in my arms.  
By a touch of yours  
My blood softly warms.  
I reach closer to your lips,  
You reach closer to mine.  
The dark fades away,  
The sun begins to shine.  
Then our lips met,  
I closed my eyes...  
A big mistake,  
I paid the price.  
A breeze swept by  
And I couldn't feel you anymore,  
I opened my eyes  
And I couldn't see you anymore.  
Love likes to play,  
Silly games with you.  
A little late did I realize,  
That the truth was never true.  
Then I closed my eyes  
And closed all the doors.  
You might not be mine,  
But I'll just be yours.
