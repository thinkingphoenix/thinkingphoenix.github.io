title: Suitor
date: 2019-05-20 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [satire, humor, matrimony, irony, societal-expectations]
---

Miss Megha is to be married.  
To save the hassle, she soughts help  
Of local newspaper matrimonies.  
Succinctly, she describes herself :  
"Homely girl with a promising job"  
With it, she rhapsodised the picture;  
A brief list that she composed with  
A precis of the suitor's features.  

She slept that night with peace of mind.  
But the next morning, a strange man came  
And knocked her door with a pearly smile.  
On his face and hand was smeared white paint.  

White hair, white coat, white tie, white shoes.  
Miss Megha was amused and surprised  
The man held out the newspaper ad  
Which said in print- "the suitor must be white".
