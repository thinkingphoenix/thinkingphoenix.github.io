---
title: Cigarettes And Pens
date: 2017-06-15 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [habits, writing, smoke, pain, expression, hindi]
---

It makes no difference  
Whether your fingers have amid them  
A cigarette or a pen.  

Either ways you exhale away  
Clouds of smoke  
Or the things you failed to say...
