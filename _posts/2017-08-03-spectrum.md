---
title: Spectrum
date: 2017-08-03 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [spectrum, emotion, music, reflection, life, memory]
---

Sometimes I feel like  
I'm splitting into a spectrum.  
At times I hold high  
My guitar's thirsty plectrum  
Emotions like flames rise  
An up-chuck follows a down-strum.  
A rhythm complements the flowing eyes  
Hurt that stings, goes numb.  

From scarlet to violet  
Rummaging for nostalgia in remains of amnesia  
This prism called life  
Is all about surviving through different media  
Or vacuum, for that matter  
Or the thought-prisons, where our dreams shatter  

And scatter, the way ants stride  
Haphazardly​, amid cookie-crumbs  
Sometimes I feel like  
I'm splitting into a spectrum.
