---
title: Gracefully Dead
date: 2025-07-25 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [death, conflict, despair]
---
My legs take steps.  
My eyes can see the world.  
Something close to a heart, beats within.  
Invisible blood stains, decorate my skin.  

Each word I state, morphs into a question.  
Each answer I give, reflects my disrepair.  
I'm something resembling a human,  
But I contradict myself with each breath.  
I flawlessly walk among the living,  
Inside, I'm gracefully dead.
