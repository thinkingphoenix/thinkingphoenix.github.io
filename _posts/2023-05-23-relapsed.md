---
title: Relapsed
date: 2023-05-23 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [addiction, pain, struggle, mental-health, isolation, despair]
---

Relapsed,  
Into the grey-hued world again.  
Relapsed,  
Into what only brings you pain.  
Relapsed,  
And no one will know.  
Relapsed,  
Your soul turning hollow.  
Relapsed,  
Under the pressure of living.  
Relapsed,  
Into the habit of mimicking.  
Relapsed,  
Into your naïve, nurturing nature.  
Relapsed,  
Into being a giver over taker.  
Relapsed,  
Into the enemies in your head.  
Relapsed,  
Until nothing of it is left.
