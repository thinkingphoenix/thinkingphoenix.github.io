---
title: Daymare
date: 2026-04-11 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [horror, internal struggle, despair]
---
I wake up with a cold fire in my chest.  
Something shattered   
Is beating inside it.  
If it mattered,   
I'd have tried to hide it.  
But they cannot see it anyway.  
This mess, that I cannot explain  
Except by breaking and  
Force-fitting words bloodstained  
Into rhymes and verses,  
The children of my curses.  
This dread I succumb to,  
Taking over me, almost visible  
Through my teeth as I smile,   
Yet luckily unnoticed by them.  
I walk along with these vile  
Hands choking my neck,  
Careful not to disturb this  
Perfect world and its beauty  
Until I find my spot in hell,   
Perhaps finally a warm place  
To rest, away from this human race.
