---
title: Unrest
date: 2018-06-08 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [dark, pain, despair, struggle, regret, unrest]
---

Cold rain seeps  
Through skin, through bone  
Through laughters, through weeps  
A slow poison, insidious  
Numbing every feeling  
He's in pretence, oblivious  
Flooded eyes, pointed at moon  
The starry night, dark bright  
Heart-pieces all around strewn.  
Words, voices, memories  
Strangle, strangle, strangle more...  
Valour, chivalry, triturated, helpless  
Until despair knocks on doors.  
Burn, burn, burn in regret...  
His armour lays broken  
His own sword in his chest.  
Forgot how it felt to feel  
Succumbs to this inner unrest.
