Do not lock eyes with me for too long. 
You may end up seeing too much.  

I preach life to you on days you embrace death.   
How ironic of me, when I'm not a child of light.  
You are, and you have constellations within your aching breast.  
Me? I would eat LEDs to glow, and be happy with it.   

In my pursuit of how life should be,  
I overwhelm my mind and I  
Disobey the God of slumber.  
I have  
a warm-hearted mother,  
Friends and siblings I hold dear.    
And I fail them not because of apathy,  
But because I keep waiting  
For the right time that never comes,  
In this automation that forever runs,  
The automation of my life.  
Because I keep waiting  
For a day when peace visits my prison.  
For this prison to be reconstructed into a mansion  
Of all that is to my name, tangible and conceptual alike.  
To break the automation of my life.  

I preach life to you on days you embrace death.   
How ironic of me, when my disrepair is in plain sight.  
But I wish I could plant flowers along your scars, the torn flesh.  
Guess what, they already bloom on you, chromatic and vivid.  
Right here, in this body you dislike the limitations of,  
That you sometimes punish for others’ sins.  
Right here, in this celestial object, barely hiding scintillations of  
The starlight emitted by the iridescent soul within.  

**B**orn from a supernova,  
**E**arth is where you reside for now.  
**A**ggrandized to me, your name is more than a proper noun.  
**U**nearthly, unbeknownst  
**T**o the crippled humankind,  
**Y**ou. Continue to blind me with brilliant light.  

And blinded I am, but I see through your form.  
Because I do not need eyes for this research.  

Hence, do not lock eyes with me for too long. 
You may end up seeing too much.
