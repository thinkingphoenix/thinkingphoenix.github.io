---
title: Sparrow
date: 2019-02-19 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [sparrow, heartbreak, survival, healing, life]
---

There came a sparrow upon my death-bed.  
She pulled out the arrow from my heart that bled.  
But before I opened my eyes  
Away she had to fly.  
And I?  
Wonder what to do with this life.
