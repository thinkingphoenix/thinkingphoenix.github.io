---
title: Arrival
date: 2023-10-30 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [love, romance, joy, bliss]
---

Like an answer to all my tears,  
Your loving hands hold my cheeks.  
And almost sceptical I stand here,  
Unable to articulate  
What your presence to me means.  

I wasn't aware he was still alive,  
The child in me who yearned for love  
But imprisoned by my will to survive.  
You nurtured him  
And broke his chains and cuffs.  

My soul back from the grave,  
Learns how to love again from you.  
From the pit of poignancy, you saved  
And painted all my darkness  
Into colors of kindness, soft hued.
