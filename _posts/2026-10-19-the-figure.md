---
title: The Figure
date: 2026-10-19 00:00:00 +0530
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [demons, internal conflict]
---

On the ceiling I see a figure.   
It has my body, my hands, my fingers.   
But his hands aren't like mine:   
My hands love, write, wipe tears from eyes.   
My hands aren't like his:   
His hands hurt, burn, show the glimpse of the abyss.   

He is on the prowl,  
His caustic saliva trickles from the maw    
And erodes my skin as it hits my body, motion-locked   
By the belts I donned    
For my own safety.   
I'm petrified. Defenseless.   
My chest heavy,    
My lungs breathless.   
My spine freezes,   
My brain releases   
Nothing. No adrenaline.   
All I could feel, atomized.    
I try to scream, agonized.    
But the voice decays in this vacuum.   
I play dead, but I'm ready to be consumed.   

Because the figure is me.   
His body has wounds from my gentle hands,   
From everytime he tried to take over   
And I clawed, punched and broke his shoulder.   
He retreated sometimes because he pitied me:   
Silently watched over my life  
And buried himself deep inside.   
Now he's been freed.   
I can beg, I can plead.   
But he will get what he needs:  
Me.   
Or rather the faint dying light I had,    
So he can feed on it and unleash his wrath.    

Because the figure is me,    
I've nowhere to hide.    
What all I've loved, will be nothingness,    
Nothing but crumbs and leftovers of this mess    
That will remain from my restructuring.    
The process seems to have begun.    
I will soon be undone.    
My soul begins to disintegrate.    
It will be over and I'll appear normal    
Before anyone can anticipate.
