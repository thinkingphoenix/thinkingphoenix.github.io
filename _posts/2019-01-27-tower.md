title: Tower
date: 2019-01-27 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [tower, struggle, victory, resilience, hardship, hindi]

Everyone sees the tower  
No one checks its base.  
Your victory will be sung but not  
The hardships you did face.  
Falling on the floor  
Riding on your feet  
Keeping your hand on your heart,  
Burning with every beat.  

Everyone sees the tower  
No one checks its base.  
Your trophies will be praised but not  
The footsteps of your race.  
Panting at every breath  
Wanting to hit that score  
Missing the mark by an inch  
Pledging to try once more.
