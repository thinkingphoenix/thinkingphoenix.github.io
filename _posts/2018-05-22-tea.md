---
title: Tea
date: 2018-05-22 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [rain, solitude, pain, sadness, longing, invitation]
---

It's raining outside,  
Just like my eyes…  

Another usual day here.  
Me, the miasma and my fears.  
On the floor, broken glass.  
Within my chest, a heavy heart.  
I don't live, but I breathe.  
No one will pick me up, nor do I plead.  

The door is open— it's raining outside.  
If you want tea…you can drop by.
