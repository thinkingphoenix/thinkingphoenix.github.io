---
title: Drunk Longing
date: 2026-08-01 00:00:00 +0000
categories: [Poetry]
tags: [longing, intoxication, yearning]
---
My occasional intoxication  
Where my mind grows loud again.  
My words are drunken,  
They stagger on the alpjhabets.  

I'm awake as my friends sleep,  
An Earth rotation since I shut my eyes,  
I bargain with slumber,  
Spelling out what's suppressed inside.   

This longing for being known  
For possibly beautiful things I'm made of.   
This longing to offer you my existence  
And assimilate your love as a trade off.  

"Love" here, being an understatement.  
As I'd prefer diving without oxygen  
Into the ocean of who you are,    
Expecting to drown, left awe-stricken.  

But I've not met you yet.  
It shatters me and I can hold on  
For no clue how long.  

I'll create crude rhymes  
And hope you read them soon.  
We're under the same Moon.  
Maybe it will guide my eyes to you?  

I want to turn you into words,  
Into pencil lines, ink and colors.  
To learn your sculpt,  
To introduce you to my camera's shutter.  

Mortal as I am, I chase this illusion  
Of eternal synchrony.  
Mortal as I am, I chase your light  
Or your darkness as retreat.  

I call into the night,  
To hear your answer as the echo.  
I will continue the fool's errand,  
I will of your shadow not let go.
